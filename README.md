# World Canons

The great works of the world's cultural traditions, built around the works themselves and the people who made them: art, architecture, philosophy, literature, music, religion, science and history.

Each tradition is its own complete section. They share the same structure, so they can be read side by side, and cross-links show where they meet: Japanese prints in Van Gogh, Aristotle preserved by Arabic scholars.

| Tradition | Status |
|---|---|
| [**Western**](western/README.md) | 8 sections (art, architecture, philosophy, literature, music, religion, science, history), 648 people and 1,101 works, with 749 pictures of works and 430 portraits |
| [**East Asian**](east-asian/README.md) | 8 sections across China, Japan and Korea: 494 people and 801 works, with 475 pictures of works and 188 portraits |
| South Asian · Islamic · Ancient Near East · African · Americas | Planned |

## How it works

Everything is generated from JSON data by `scripts/build.py`, which uses only the Python standard library. Images come from Wikimedia Commons; works still under copyright link to the official museum or collection page instead. See [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md) for the data format and how to add to it.
