## Biography

Little is known of Guan Hanqing's life. He lived in the Yuan capital Dadu, probably worked as a physician or in the medical bureau, belonged to a writers' guild (shuhui), and is said to have acted on stage himself. He wrote for the professional actresses and troupes of the capital.

His plays range from the tragedy The Injustice to Dou E to comedies about clever courtesans and historical plays about the Three Kingdoms. Several, especially The Single Sword Meeting, have survived on the stage through kunqu and later opera. The plays and his literary achievement are treated in the literature section.

## Key works & forms

### The Single Sword Meeting (Dandao hui) (13th century)

- **Date:** 13th century
- **Type:** Zaju play
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=The%20Single%20Sword%20Meeting%20%28Dandao%20hui%29%20Guan%20Hanqing)

In this historical zaju, Lu Su of Wu invites Guan Yu to a banquet in order to force the return of Jingzhou, but Guan Yu crosses the river with only a single sword and so overawes his hosts that he returns unharmed. The fourth act contains the famous arias sung by Guan Yu as he crosses the Yangtze, looking back on the battle of the Red Cliffs and the blood of heroes in the river. The scene survived on the kunqu stage as 'Dao hui' and was taken into Peking opera, keeping Guan Hanqing's words alive in performance for seven centuries.
