# He Zhanhao

*何占豪*

**1933** · Chinese  
Born: Zhuji, Zhejiang · Died:   
Genres & eras: [Postwar & avant-garde](../movements/postwar-avant-garde.md)  

> Composer and violinist who wrote The Butterfly Lovers Violin Concerto with Chen Gang as students at the Shanghai Conservatory.

## Biography

He Zhanhao played in a Yue opera troupe before entering the Shanghai Conservatory, where he was in a student group exploring ways to play Chinese music on the violin. In 1958 he and fellow student Chen Gang (1935–2024) began a concerto on a story from Yue opera, completed in 1959.

The Butterfly Lovers became the most popular Chinese orchestral work, and He went on to compose for the Chinese orchestra, including concertos for guzheng and erhu. The concerto's shared authorship, with Chen Gang responsible for much of the orchestration, is acknowledged in its credits.

## Key works & forms

### The Butterfly Lovers Violin Concerto (1959)

- **Date:** 1959
- **Type:** Violin concerto
- **Where performed / held:** Shanghai Conservatory (premiere May 1959, soloist Yu Lina)
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=The%20Butterfly%20Lovers%20Violin%20Concerto%20He%20Zhanhao)

The concerto tells the folk legend of Zhu Yingtai, who disguises herself as a man to study and falls in love with her classmate Liang Shanbo, who dies of grief when she is promised to another; at his grave she joins him in death, and the lovers turn into butterflies. He Zhanhao and Chen Gang wrote it as students at the Shanghai Conservatory for the tenth anniversary of the People's Republic, basing its themes on the melodies of Yue opera. The single-movement work uses Romantic Western forms while the violin imitates the slides and ornaments of Chinese fiddles and singers. It is the most recorded Chinese orchestral piece. It is still in copyright.
