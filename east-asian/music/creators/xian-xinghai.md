# Xian Xinghai

*冼星海*

**1905 – 1945** · Chinese  
Born: Macau · Died: Moscow  
Genres & eras: [Western-style composers](../movements/modern-composers.md)  

> Composer of the Yellow River Cantata, the most famous Chinese patriotic work of the war against Japan.

## Biography

Xian Xinghai was born in Macau to a poor fisherman's family and studied music in Guangzhou, Beijing and Shanghai. From 1929 to 1935 he studied in Paris, working at odd jobs, and was accepted into the Paris Conservatoire. Back in China he wrote songs for the anti-Japanese movement, and in 1938 he went to the Communist base at Yan'an to teach at the Lu Xun Academy of Arts. Sent to the Soviet Union in 1940, he was stranded there by the German invasion, fell ill and died in Moscow in 1945.

His music combines Chinese folk melody with Western forms in a direct, popular style intended for mass singing. Besides the Yellow River Cantata he wrote hundreds of songs, symphonies and other works, and he became a national hero in the People's Republic.

## Key works & forms

### Yellow River Cantata (1939)

- **Date:** 1939
- **Type:** Cantata
- **Where performed / held:** Yan'an (premiere April 1939)
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Yellow%20River%20Cantata%20Xian%20Xinghai)

The poet Guang Weiran wrote the text after crossing the Yellow River with a resistance troupe, and Xian Xinghai composed the music in a few days in Yan'an in March 1939, for the small forces available, including Chinese instruments. Its eight movements include the 'Song of the Yellow River Boatmen', an ode to the river, a lament and the climactic 'Defend the Yellow River', a round for mass singing, and 'Roar, Yellow River'. Revised by Xian in Moscow and later arranged for full orchestra, it became one of the defining works of modern Chinese music. In 1969 a group of composers led by Yin Chengzong reworked it as the Yellow River Piano Concerto, which became internationally famous.
