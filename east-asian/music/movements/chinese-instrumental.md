# Chinese folk & solo instrumental music (民族器樂)

*c. 1800 – present*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Alongside opera, regional ensemble and solo traditions flourished in late imperial China: pipa suites printed from 1818, silk-and-bamboo ensembles of the south, and the two-string erhu fiddle raised to a concert instrument in the early twentieth century by Liu Tianhua and the blind street musician Abing.
