# Pansori & sanjo (판소리·산조)

*c. 1700 – present*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Pansori is Korean epic storytelling by one singer and one drummer, a performance that can last several hours, sung in a husky, strained voice. Of the twelve stories once sung, five survive in full. Sanjo, 'scattered melodies', is its instrumental counterpart, a solo for gayageum or other instrument that accelerates through a series of rhythmic cycles.
