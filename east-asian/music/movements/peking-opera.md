# Peking opera (京劇)

*1790 – present*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Peking opera formed after 1790, when troupes from Anhui came to Beijing for the Qianlong emperor's birthday and merged their styles with Hubei and kunqu traditions. It combines song, speech, acrobatics and mime with painted faces and conventional role types, accompanied by the jinghu fiddle and percussion. Mei Lanfang made it internationally famous in the twentieth century.
