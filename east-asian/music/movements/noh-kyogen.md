# Noh & kyōgen (能・狂言)

*c. 1370 – present*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

Noh grew out of sarugaku entertainments under Kan'ami and his son Zeami, patronised by the shogun Ashikaga Yoshimitsu. A masked lead actor, a chorus, flute and three drums perform slow, distilled plays about gods, warriors, women and ghosts on a bare cypress stage. Comic kyōgen pieces are played between them. The repertory and style were fixed in the Edo period and remain largely unchanged.
