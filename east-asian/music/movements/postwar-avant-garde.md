# Postwar & avant-garde (現代音楽)

*1945 – present*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/east-asian-contemporary.md)

After 1945 East Asian composers joined the international avant-garde while rediscovering their own traditions: Takemitsu combined orchestra with biwa and shakuhachi, Isang Yun built a Western modernism on Korean court music, Hwang Byungki wrote new gayageum music, and Chinese composers moved from revolutionary works to a cosmopolitan style after 1978.
