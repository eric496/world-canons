# Koto & shamisen music (箏曲・地歌)

*c. 1600–1868*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Blind musicians of the Tōdō guild, holding ranks such as kengyō, created the chamber music of Edo Japan for koto zither and the three-string shamisen. Yatsuhashi Kengyō founded the modern koto tradition with instrumental variations (danmono) and songs; later masters wrote long jiuta pieces with instrumental interludes. Shamisen styles also drove kabuki and puppet theatre.
