# Biwa narrative & Buddhist chant (平曲)

*c. 1200–1600*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Blind priests called biwa hōshi chanted The Tale of the Heike to the lute, a style later called heikyoku; Buddhist temples developed shōmyō chant. These narrative and liturgical traditions shaped the vocal style of Noh and later Japanese song.
