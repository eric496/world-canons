# Han Music Bureau & early song (漢樂府)

*c. 120 BC – AD 589*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

Emperor Wu of Han enlarged the Music Bureau (Yuefu), which collected folk songs and composed music for state sacrifices and banquets; its name became that of a whole genre of poetry. Through the Six Dynasties new instruments arrived from Central Asia, and literati poets wrote verses to be sung, though almost none of the melodies survive.
