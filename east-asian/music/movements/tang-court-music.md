# Tang court music & Silk Road instruments (唐代燕樂)

*618–907*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

The Tang court maintained huge ensembles playing banquet music (yanyue) in ten official styles, several of them foreign, from Kucha, Bukhara, India and Korea. The pear-shaped pipa, the ruan and harps came along the Silk Road. Emperor Xuanzong trained musicians in the Pear Garden. Much of this repertory is lost in China but survives, much changed, in Japanese gagaku and in instruments preserved in the Shōsōin.
