# Korean aak & hyangak (아악·향악)

*1116 – present*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

Korea received Chinese ritual music (aak) from Song China in 1116. Under King Sejong in the fifteenth century Bak Yeon rebuilt the ritual orchestra and its instruments, while the king composed new music in native style (hyangak) and devised the jeongganbo notation. The Jongmyo shrine music, still performed each year, and suites like Sujecheon are the core of the court repertory.
