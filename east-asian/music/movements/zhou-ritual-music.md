# Zhou ritual music & bell sets (周代雅樂)

*c. 1046–221 BC*

Era: [Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools](../../periods/early-dynasties.md)

Under the Zhou kings music was part of ritual (li), and the Confucian classics treat 'rites and music' as the twin foundations of social order. Aristocratic courts played tuned sets of bronze bells and stone chimes, ranked by status, with zithers, mouth organs and drums. The bells from the tomb of Marquis Yi of Zeng show how sophisticated the theory of pitch had become by the fifth century BC.
