# Western-style composers (近代作曲家)

*1868–1945*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

Meiji Japan introduced Western music through school songs and military bands, and the first Japanese composers set Japanese poems to Western harmony. In China and Korea similar songs, anthems and cantatas grew up in a time of national crisis, war and revolution, while traditional players such as Miyagi Michio adapted their instruments to the new ideas.
