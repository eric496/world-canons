# Song ci songs & court revival (宋詞樂)

*960–1279*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Song dynasty ci lyrics were written to existing tunes, sung in pleasure quarters and at literati gatherings. Emperor Huizong tried to restore ancient ritual music with new bell sets, and the poet-composer Jiang Kui left the only sizeable set of Song songs with their notation.
