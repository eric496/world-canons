# Bunraku & kabuki (文楽・歌舞伎)

*1603 – present*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Two theatres of townsmen's Edo Japan. Kabuki began with Izumo no Okuni's dances in Kyoto in 1603 and became an all-male theatre of star actors, bold make-up and stylised poses. Puppet theatre (ningyō jōruri, now called bunraku) set narrative chanting to the shamisen, and its great plays by Chikamatsu and later authors were quickly adapted for kabuki. Ukiyo-e actor prints recorded both.
