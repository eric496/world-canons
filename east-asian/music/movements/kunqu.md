# Ming chuanqi & kunqu (崑曲)

*c. 1550–1800*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

In the sixteenth century Wei Liangfu and his circle in Kunshan refined a slow, ornate singing style accompanied by the bamboo flute. Kunqu became the national elite theatre for two centuries, performing long southern chuanqi plays such as The Peony Pavilion and The Palace of Eternal Youth. Prized for its poetry, dance and refinement, it is the ancestor of many later Chinese operas.
