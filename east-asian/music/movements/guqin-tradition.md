# The guqin tradition (古琴)

*c. 500 BC – present*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

The seven-string zither was the instrument of the Chinese scholar, played in private for self-cultivation rather than for an audience. Legends tie its repertory to Confucius, Boya and Ji Kang. Its tablature records finger positions and strokes but not rhythm, so each player must reconstruct ('dapu') a piece; about three thousand versions of some six hundred pieces survive in handbooks from the Ming onward.
