# Yuan zaju (元雜劇)

*c. 1260–1368*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Under Mongol rule, northern 'variety plays' (zaju) became the first mature Chinese theatre: four acts of spoken dialogue and arias sung by a single lead to fixed tune patterns in one mode per act. Guan Hanqing and Wang Shifu are its masters; the plays themselves are treated in the literature section.
