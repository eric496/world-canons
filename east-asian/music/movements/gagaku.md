# Japanese gagaku & bugaku (雅楽)

*c. 700 – present*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Gagaku, 'elegant music', is the court music of Japan, built from Tang Chinese (tōgaku) and Korean (komagaku) repertories imported in the seventh to ninth centuries and from native Shinto song. It was standardised in the Heian period and has been played by hereditary families ever since, today by the Music Department of the Imperial Household Agency. Bugaku is its masked dance form. It is one of the oldest continuously performed orchestral traditions in the world.
