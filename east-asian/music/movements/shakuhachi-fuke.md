# Shakuhachi & the Fuke sect (尺八・普化宗)

*c. 1600–1871*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

In the Edo period wandering komusō monks of the Fuke Zen sect, faces hidden in basket hats, played the end-blown bamboo shakuhachi as a meditation practice (suizen). Their solo pieces, honkyoku, are slow and breath-centred. The sect was abolished in 1871, but schools such as the Kinko-ryū preserved the repertory as art music.
