# Wang Shifu

*王實甫*

**fl. late 13th century** · Chinese  
Born: Dadu (Beijing) · Died:   
Movements & eras: [Yuan drama](../movements/yuan-drama.md)  

> Yuan dramatist and author of The Romance of the Western Chamber, the most famous Chinese love comedy.

## Biography

Almost nothing is known of Wang Shifu's life beyond his origin in Dadu and a list of fourteen plays attributed to him, of which three survive. He worked in the same theatrical world as Guan Hanqing, and some later editors even suggested that Guan completed the last part of the Western Chamber, a claim now generally rejected.

Wang's fame rests on The Romance of the Western Chamber, a play of exceptional length in five parts whose lyrical arias were praised as the most beautiful in Yuan drama. Its sympathetic portrait of young lovers defying parental authority made it both cherished and censured for centuries.

- **Date:** c. 1300
- **Type:** Zaju drama (five parts)
- **Written / first published:** Dadu (Beijing)
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=The%20Romance%20of%20the%20Western%20Chamber%20Wang%20Shifu)

The story goes back to Yuan Zhen's Tang tale of Yingying, in which the lover abandons the heroine, and to a Jin-dynasty medley by Master Dong that gave it a happy ending. Wang Shifu's version, in an unusual twenty-one acts, tells how the young scholar Zhang Gong meets Cui Yingying in the Monastery of Universal Salvation, where she is travelling with her widowed mother. When bandits besiege the temple, the mother promises Yingying to whoever saves them; Zhang summons help, but the mother breaks her word. With the help of the maid Hongniang, who carries letters and defies her mistress, the lovers meet in secret, and Zhang finally passes the examinations and wins Yingying. Its lyrical arias and praise of love freely chosen made it immensely popular and controversial; it is the book Baoyu and Daiyu read together in Dream of the Red Chamber, and Hongniang's name became a word for a matchmaker.
