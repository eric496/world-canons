# Ba Jin

*巴金*

**1904 – 2005** · Chinese  
Born: Chengdu, Sichuan · Died: Shanghai  
Movements & eras: [May Fourth & modern Chinese literature](../movements/may-fourth-modern.md)  

> Novelist whose Family attacked the patriarchal household and inspired a generation of young readers.

## Biography

Ba Jin, the pen name of Li Yaotang, grew up in a large, wealthy gentry family in Chengdu, the model for the household of his novels. He left in 1923, studied in Shanghai and Nanjing and then in France, and was drawn to anarchism; his pen name has been linked to Bakunin and Kropotkin. He was persecuted during the Cultural Revolution.

Family (1933), the first part of his Turbulent Stream trilogy, depicts young people struggling against the authority of their grandfather and arranged marriages, and became a manifesto for youth rebelling against tradition. In old age his Random Thoughts, a series of essays examining his own conduct during the Cultural Revolution, made him a moral voice in China.

## Key works

### Family (1933)

- **Date:** 1933
- **Type:** Novel
- **Written / first published:** Serialised in Shanghai, 1931–32; book 1933
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Family%20Ba%20Jin)

Set in Chengdu around 1920, Family follows the brothers of the Gao household. The eldest, Juexin, gives up his studies and the cousin he loves to accept an arranged marriage and run the family business, then sees his wife die in childbirth outside the house because of superstition. The second, Juemin, defies the family to marry his cousin. The youngest, Juehui, a student inspired by the May Fourth movement, loves the maid Mingfeng, who drowns herself when she is given as a concubine to an old man, and at the end he leaves for Shanghai. The novel's indictment of the patriarchal family moved generations of young readers; it was followed by Spring and Autumn to form the Turbulent Stream trilogy.
