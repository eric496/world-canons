# Yuan drama (元雜劇)

*1271–1368*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Under Mongol rule, when the civil-service examinations were suspended and many scholars lost their careers, writers turned to the theatre. Zaju, four-act plays in which one lead role sings arias in each act, became China's first mature drama. Guan Hanqing wrote of injustice and resourceful women, Wang Shifu of romance, and Ma Zhiyuan of melancholy scholars; their colloquial lyrics (sanqu) were a new poetry in themselves.
