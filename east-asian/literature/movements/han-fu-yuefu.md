# Han rhapsody & Music Bureau songs (漢賦 · 樂府)

*c. 200 BC – AD 220*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

The Han court favoured the fu or rhapsody, an extravagant mix of prose and rhyme that catalogued hunting parks, palaces and capitals in dazzling vocabulary; Sima Xiangru was its master. At the same time the imperial Music Bureau (yuefu) collected and composed popular ballads, whose plain narrative style and five-character line, refined in the anonymous Nineteen Old Poems, set the pattern for classical shi poetry.

## Authors

-  [Sima Xiangru](../creators/sima-xiangru.md) (c. 179 BC – 117 BC)
-  [Han Music Bureau poets](../creators/yuefu-poets.md) (c. 2nd century BC – c. 3rd century AD)
