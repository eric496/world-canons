# Meiji to early Shōwa modern literature (近代文学)

*1868–1945*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

Opening to the West brought translations, newspapers and a new written language closer to speech (genbun itchi). Mori Ōgai and Natsume Sōseki created the modern Japanese novel of the self; Higuchi Ichiyō wrote of Tokyo's poor in a last classical style; Masaoka Shiki reformed haiku and tanka. In the Taishō and early Shōwa years Akutagawa perfected the short story, while Tanizaki and Kawabata began careers that spanned the war.
