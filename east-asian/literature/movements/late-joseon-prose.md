# Late Joseon pansori & Silhak prose (판소리 · 實學)

*1700–1897*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

In the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries Korean literature broadened beyond the yangban elite. Pansori singers performed long sung stories such as Chunhyang and Sim Cheong for village and court audiences, and these spread as printed Hangul novels. Practical-learning (Silhak) scholars such as Pak Ji-won wrote satirical tales and travel diaries in a lively Chinese, and court women produced memoirs in Hangul, above all Lady Hyegyeong.
