# Edo literature (江戸文学)

*1603–1868*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Peace and prosperous cities under the Tokugawa shogunate created a commercial literature for merchants as well as samurai. Ihara Saikaku wrote witty 'books of the floating world'; Chikamatsu Monzaemon wrote plays for puppet (jōruri) and kabuki theatres; Matsuo Bashō transformed linked verse into haikai poetry and travel journals, followed by Buson and Issa. Later came supernatural tales, comic travel novels and Bakin's vast historical romances.
