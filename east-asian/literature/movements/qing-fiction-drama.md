# Qing fiction & drama (清代小說)

*1644–1911*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

The Qing dynasty produced the most refined Chinese novels and plays. Pu Songling wrote classical-language tales of ghosts and fox spirits, Wu Jingzi satirised the examination system in The Scholars, and Cao Xueqin's Dream of the Red Chamber portrayed the decline of a great family with unmatched depth. Kong Shangren and Hong Sheng wrote the last great chuanqi dramas, and Shen Fu's memoir opened an intimate personal prose.
