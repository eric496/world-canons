# Postwar Japanese literature (戦後文学)

*1945–1990s*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/east-asian-contemporary.md)

Defeat, occupation and recovery shaped a generation of writers. Dazai Osamu voiced the despair of the immediate postwar years; Tanizaki and Kawabata wrote late masterpieces of a vanishing Japan, and Kawabata became the first Japanese Nobel laureate in 1968; Mishima Yukio fused aestheticism and nationalism; and Ōe Kenzaburō wrote of the atomic bomb, disability and responsibility, winning the Nobel Prize in 1994.
