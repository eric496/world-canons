# Ming novels & chuanqi drama (明代小說 · 傳奇)

*1368–1644*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

Storytellers' cycles about heroes, rebels and pilgrims were shaped into long vernacular novels: Romance of the Three Kingdoms, Water Margin, Journey to the West and the startlingly frank Jin Ping Mei, later called the Four Masterworks of the Ming. Printing spread them widely. On stage, long southern chuanqi plays replaced zaju, culminating in Tang Xianzu's Peony Pavilion, while Feng Menglong's collections made the vernacular short story a literary form.
