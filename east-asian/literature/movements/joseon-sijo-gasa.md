# Joseon sijo, gasa & early Hangul fiction (時調 · 歌辭)

*1392–1700*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

The invention of Hangul in 1443 let Koreans write their own language easily, though classical Chinese remained the language of scholarship. The sijo, a three-line sung poem, was cultivated by Confucian officials and kisaeng entertainers alike, Hwang Jini above all; the longer gasa was perfected by Jeong Cheol. Prose fiction began with Kim Si-seup's tales in Chinese and grew into Hangul novels such as The Tale of Hong Gildong and Kim Man-jung's Cloud Dream of the Nine.
