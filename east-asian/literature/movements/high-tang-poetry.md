# High Tang poetry (盛唐詩)

*c. 700–770*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

The first half of the eighth century, under Emperor Xuanzong, is regarded as the summit of Chinese poetry. Regulated verse with strict tonal patterns reached perfection alongside freer old-style ballads. Li Bai brought Daoist abandon and wine, Wang Wei a Buddhist stillness in landscape, and Du Fu, writing through the catastrophe of the An Lushan rebellion (755–763), a moral seriousness and technical range later ages treated as the standard.
