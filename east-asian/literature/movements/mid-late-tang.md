# Mid & late Tang poetry and guwen prose (中晚唐 · 古文)

*c. 770–907*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

After the rebellion, poetry diversified. Bai Juyi wrote plain, socially critical ballads meant to be understood by anyone; Han Yu and Liu Zongyuan led the guwen ('ancient prose') movement, rejecting ornate parallel prose for the direct style of the classics; and in the late Tang Li Shangyin and Du Mu wrote dense, allusive and ambiguous verse. Tang tales (chuanqi) of love and the supernatural also flourished.
