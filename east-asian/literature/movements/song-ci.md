# Song ci and Song letters (宋詞)

*960–1279*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

The ci, lyrics written to existing tunes with lines of uneven length, began in the entertainment quarters of the Tang and became the signature form of the Song dynasty. Su Shi widened it from love and parting to philosophy and history, Li Qingzhao gave it its most delicate personal voice, and Xin Qiji and Lu You filled it with patriotic grief after the loss of the north to the Jurchen. Song prose, led by Ouyang Xiu and Su Shi, perfected the familiar essay.
