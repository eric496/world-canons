# Six Dynasties poetry (六朝詩)

*c. 220–589*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

In the centuries of division after the Han, poets turned inward and toward nature. Cao Zhi and the Jian'an poets wrote of war and loss; Tao Yuanming made farming, wine and retirement into poetry; Xie Lingyun invented the landscape poem of mountains and rivers. Southern courts polished parallel prose, tonal rules and anthologies such as the Wenxuan, laying the technical foundations of Tang verse.
