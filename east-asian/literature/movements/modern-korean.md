# Modern Korean literature (韓國現代文學)

*1906–1950*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

New-style fiction and poetry in Hangul emerged at the end of Joseon and grew under Japanese colonial rule (1910–45). Yi Kwang-su's Mujeong (1917) is counted the first modern Korean novel; Han Yong-un and Kim Sowol wrote lyric poetry of loss and longing; Yi Sang experimented with modernist fiction and verse; and Yun Dong-ju, who died in a Japanese prison, became the poet of conscience of the colonial era.
