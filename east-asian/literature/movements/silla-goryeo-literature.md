# Silla hyangga & Goryeo literature (鄕歌 · 高麗文學)

*c. 600–1392*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Early Korean literature was written in two ways: in classical Chinese, the shared learned language of East Asia, by scholars such as Choe Chiwon who studied in Tang China; and as hyangga, 'native songs' in Korean recorded with Chinese characters used for sound and meaning. Only 25 hyangga survive, mostly Buddhist poems preserved in the Samguk yusa and a Goryeo biography. Goryeo added songs (gayo), anecdotal prose and the first stirrings of fiction.
