# May Fourth & modern Chinese literature (五四新文學)

*1917–1949*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

The New Culture movement, crystallised in the student protests of 4 May 1919, rejected classical Chinese for baihua, the written vernacular, and Confucian tradition for science and democracy. Lu Xun's short stories inaugurated modern Chinese fiction; Mao Dun, Ba Jin, Lao She and Shen Congwen wrote novels of a society in upheaval; and in occupied Shanghai Eileen Chang wrote stories of love and survival. After 1949 literature in the mainland was subordinated to politics.
