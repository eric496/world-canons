# Nara chronicles & the Man'yōshū (奈良文学)

*c. 700–800*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Japan's written literature begins in the eighth century with works that adapted Chinese characters to the Japanese language. The Kojiki (712) and Nihon shoki (720) set down myths and royal genealogies, the Kaifūsō collected poems in Chinese, and the Man'yōshū gathered some 4,500 Japanese poems, from long chōka by court poets such as Kakinomoto no Hitomaro to frontier guards' songs, written in the phonetic man'yōgana script.
