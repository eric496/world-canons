# Heian court literature (平安文学)

*c. 900–1185*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

At the Heian court in Kyoto, the new kana syllabary allowed Japanese to be written fluently, and aristocratic women created one of the world's first great prose literatures. Imperial anthologies of waka beginning with the Kokinshū (905) set poetic taste for a thousand years; diaries, poem-tales and the Pillow Book recorded court life; and Murasaki Shikibu's Tale of Genji turned the monogatari into a psychological novel of immense scope.
