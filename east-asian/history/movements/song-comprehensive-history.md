# Song comprehensive & institutional history (宋代史學)

*960–1320*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Song scholars, working with printing and a vastly expanded literate elite, reshaped Chinese history. Ouyang Xiu rewrote the Tang and Five Dynasties histories with sharper moral judgement; Sima Guang's Comprehensive Mirror told 1,362 years in one chronological narrative; Zhu Xi's school condensed it into a moral outline; and Ma Duanlin's encyclopedia traced institutions across the ages.
