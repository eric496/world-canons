# Three Kingdoms & Goryeo histories (삼국·고려 사서)

*414–1300*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Korea's earliest surviving historical text is the Gwanggaeto Stele of 414. Kim Busik's official Samguk sagi of 1145, in the Chinese composite form, and the monk Iryeon's Samguk yusa, with its myths, Buddhist legends and songs, are the twin foundations of Korean history for the Three Kingdoms period.

## Historians & authors

-  [King Jangsu of Goguryeo](../creators/jangsu.md) (394 – 491)
-  [Kim Busik](../creators/kim-busik.md) (1075 – 1151)
-  [Iryeon](../creators/iryeon.md) (1206 – 1289)
