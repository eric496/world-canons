# Joseon Veritable Records & court diaries (조선 실록과 일기)

*1392–1910*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

Joseon made record-keeping a moral and political institution. Independent historians recorded every royal audience for the Veritable Records; the Royal Secretariat kept a daily diary; King Sejong's scholars created the Hangul alphabet and compiled the History of Goryeo. The Japanese invasions of the 1590s produced two classics, Yi Sun-sin's war diary and Ryu Seong-ryong's Jingbirok, and Silhak scholars later rewrote Korean history critically.
