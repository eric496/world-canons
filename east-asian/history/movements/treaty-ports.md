# Opium War, treaties & Western learning (鴉片戰爭與西學)

*1839–1870*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

The Opium War brought East Asia into the treaty system of the Western powers. Lin Zexu's campaign against opium, the Treaty of Nanking and Wei Yuan's study of the maritime nations mark China's encounter; in Japan Fukuzawa Yukichi's Conditions in the West explained Western institutions to a wide readership on the eve of the Meiji Restoration.
