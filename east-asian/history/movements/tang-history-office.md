# Tang History Office & the codes (唐代史館與律令)

*618–907*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

The Tang made history-writing a function of the state: a History Office compiled veritable records and the histories of earlier dynasties by committee, establishing the official series later called the Twenty-Four Histories. The same state produced the Tang Code, the legal model for East Asia, while Liu Zhiji's Shitong subjected the whole enterprise to criticism and Du You's Tongdian began institutional history.
