# Edo historiography (江戸の史学)

*1644–1868*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Under Tokugawa peace, Confucian scholars wrote Japanese history in Chinese forms. The shogunate's Hayashi school produced the Honchō tsugan; the Mito domain began the Dai Nihonshi, whose loyalism fed the late Edo imperial movement; Arai Hakuseki explained the rise of the warriors rationally; and Rai San'yō's Nihon gaishi became the best-selling history of the age.
