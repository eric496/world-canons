# Revolution, nationalism & the new history (革命與新史學)

*1902–1945*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

The end of empire in China and the colonisation of Korea produced new documents and new histories. Liang Qichao called for a history of the nation instead of dynasties; the Qing abdicated in 1912 and Sun Yat-sen set out his Three Principles; Korean leaders declared independence in 1919 while Sin Chae-ho wrote a nationalist history; Gu Jiegang's sceptics questioned the whole of ancient tradition.
