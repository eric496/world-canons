# Ming & Qing official histories (明清官修史書)

*1368–1912*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

Each dynasty wrote the history of its predecessor. The Ming produced the History of Yuan in two hurried years; the Qing laboured for sixty years over the History of Ming. Together with the earlier histories these were canonised in the eighteenth century as the Twenty-Four Histories.
