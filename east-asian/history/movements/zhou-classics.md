# Zhou classics & annals (先秦史書)

*c. 1000–221 BC*

Era: [Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools](../../periods/early-dynasties.md)

Chinese history-writing begins with court scribes whose records of speeches, decrees and events were gathered into the Book of Documents and the terse Spring and Autumn Annals of the state of Lu. In the Warring States period the Zuo Commentary and the Discourses of the States turned those entries into vivid narrative, setting the patterns of moral judgement and dramatic speech that later historians inherited.
