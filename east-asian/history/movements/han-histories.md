# Han historians & the early standard histories (漢魏六朝史學)

*c. 100 BC – AD 500*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

Sima Qian's Records of the Grand Historian created the composite form of annals, tables, treatises and biographies; Ban Gu's Book of Han narrowed it to a single dynasty and so created the dynastic history. Chen Shou and Fan Ye extended the series to the Three Kingdoms and the Eastern Han, and Ban Zhao became China's first woman historian.
