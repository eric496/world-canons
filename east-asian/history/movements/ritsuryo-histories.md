# Ritsuryō codes & the Six National Histories (律令と六国史)

*701–901*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Adopting Chinese models, the Japanese court issued the Taihō Code of 701 and compiled official histories in classical Chinese, beginning with the Nihon Shoki in 720. Six such histories (Rikkokushi) carry the court's record down to 887, after which official history-writing lapsed.
