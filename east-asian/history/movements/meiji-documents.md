# Meiji constitutionalism & modern historiography (明治の国家と史学)

*1868–1912*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

The Meiji state announced its programme in the Charter Oath of 1868 and gave itself a written constitution in 1889, modelled largely on Prussia. Government historians and university scholars such as Kume Kunitake introduced Western source criticism, at times clashing with official orthodoxy.
