# Postwar constitutions (戦後の憲法)

*1945–present*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/east-asian-contemporary.md)

Defeat and occupation produced the Constitution of Japan of 1946, with its renunciation of war and its guarantee of fundamental rights, the most consequential East Asian founding document since 1945.
