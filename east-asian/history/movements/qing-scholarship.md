# Qing scholarship & evidential history (清代考據史學)

*1644–1850*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Reacting to the fall of the Ming, Qing scholars such as Gu Yanwu turned to evidential research (kaozheng), testing texts against inscriptions, geography and philology. The Qianlong court's vast Siku quanshu both preserved and censored the written heritage, and Zhang Xuecheng argued that the classics themselves were history.
