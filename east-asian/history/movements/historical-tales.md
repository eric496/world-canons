# Historical tales & medieval interpretations (歴史物語と史論)

*c. 1100–1350*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

After the end of the official histories, Japanese writers told the past in vernacular 'historical tales' such as the Ōkagami, while the Kamakura shogunate kept its own chronicle, the Azuma Kagami. Two works tried to explain the whole course of Japanese history: Jien's Gukanshō, through Buddhist ideas of principle and decline, and Kitabatake Chikafusa's Jinnō Shōtōki, through the divine imperial succession.
