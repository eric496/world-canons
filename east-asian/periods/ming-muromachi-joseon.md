# Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon

*1368 – 1644*

Ming China's Forbidden City, porcelain, novels and Wang Yangming; Zen culture, Noh theatre and the tea ceremony in Muromachi and Momoyama Japan; Joseon Korea invents the Hangul alphabet.
