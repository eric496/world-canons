# Song, Heian & Goryeo

*960 – 1368*

Song China: printing, Neo-Confucianism, monumental landscape painting and the civil-service state, followed by Mongol Yuan rule; Heian court literature (The Tale of Genji) and the warrior Kamakura era in Japan; Goryeo celadon and the Tripitaka Koreana in Korea.
