# The Cosmopolitan Age

*589 – 960*

Sui and Tang China, the age of Li Bai and Du Fu, Buddhist cave temples and the Silk Road; Japan adopts Chinese writing, law and Buddhism in the Asuka, Nara and early Heian periods; Unified Silla in Korea.
