# Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools

*c. 1600 – 221 BC*

Shang oracle bones and bronzes; the Zhou dynasty and its classics; in the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods Confucius, Laozi, Mozi, Mencius, Zhuangzi and Han Feizi found Chinese philosophy. Jōmon and Yayoi cultures in Japan; Gojoseon in Korea.
