# Qing, Edo & Late Joseon

*1644 – 1868*

The Manchu Qing dynasty at its height and Dream of the Red Chamber; Tokugawa Japan's peace, kabuki, haiku and ukiyo-e; true-view landscape painting and Silhak practical learning in Korea. Ends with Western gunboats and the opening of Japan.
