# The First Empires

*221 BC – AD 589*

Qin unification and the Terracotta Army; the Han dynasty makes Confucianism state orthodoxy, writes the first standard history and invents paper; Buddhism arrives from India; the Six Dynasties produce calligraphy and landscape poetry. Kofun-era Japan and the Three Kingdoms of Korea.
