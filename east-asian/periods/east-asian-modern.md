# Revolution & Modernisation

*1868 – 1945*

Meiji Japan modernises and builds an empire; the Qing falls in 1911 and the May Fourth movement remakes Chinese culture; Korea is annexed by Japan in 1910. New literatures, art schools and ideas meet the West, ending in war.
