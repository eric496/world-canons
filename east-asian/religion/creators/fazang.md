## Biography

Fazang's family was of Sogdian origin, settled in Chang'an. He studied under Zhiyan, joined Xuanzang's translation bureau for a time, and was ordained only in 670 when Empress Wu Zetian founded a monastery in memory of her mother. He took part in the new eighty-fascicle translation of the Avataṃsaka Sutra completed in 699 and lectured on it at court.

Fazang's teaching that each phenomenon contains and is contained by all others, famously illustrated with a golden lion and a hall of mirrors, is treated in the philosophy section. As a religious figure he was a court preacher, a performer of rituals for rain and victory, and the third patriarch of Huayan; his letter to the Korean monk Uisang testifies to the close links of East Asian Buddhism.
