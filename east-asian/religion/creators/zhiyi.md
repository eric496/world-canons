## Biography

Zhiyi became a monk after his family was ruined in the wars of the Liang collapse and studied under Huisi, who taught him a meditation based on the Lotus Sutra. He lectured in the Chen capital Jiankang and in 575 withdrew to Mount Tiantai, which gave the school its name. Patronised by the Sui prince Yang Guang, later Emperor Yang, he received the title Master of Wisdom (Zhizhe).

Zhiyi's lectures, recorded by his disciple Guanding, classified the scriptures into periods and teachings, set out the threefold truth of emptiness, provisional existence and the middle, and taught calming and insight meditation. He also composed repentance rituals that remain in use. The philosophy section treats his doctrinal works; as a religious founder he gave East Asia its first comprehensive Buddhist school and, through Saichō, the basis of Japanese Tendai.

## Key texts & works

### Lotus Samādhi Repentance (Fahua sanmei chanyi) (6th century)

- **Date:** 6th century
- **Type:** Ritual and meditation manual
- **Where written / held:** Mount Tiantai, Zhejiang
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Lotus%20Sam%C4%81dhi%20Repentance%20%28Fahua%20sanmei%20chanyi%29%20Zhiyi)

Zhiyi prescribed four kinds of samādhi practice, of which the Lotus Samādhi combined sitting and walking meditation. This manual sets out its stages: purifying the sanctuary, offerings, invocation of buddhas and of the bodhisattva Samantabhadra, confession of the sins of the six senses, circumambulation, recitation of the Lotus Sutra and seated contemplation of the real aspect of things. It shows how Tiantai joined doctrine to ritual and penance. Rites based on it were performed on Mount Hiei and are still held in Tendai temples.
