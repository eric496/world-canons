# Confucian ritual and the state cult (禮教)

*c. 500 BC – 1910s*

Era: [Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools](../../periods/early-dynasties.md)

Confucius and his followers treated ritual (li) as the backbone of moral and social life: mourning, ancestor offerings, family ceremonies and the great sacrifices of the state to Heaven, Earth and the dynastic ancestors. From the Han onward emperors, and later the kings of Joseon Korea, performed these rites as a duty of rule, and Confucius himself received sacrifice in temples across East Asia. Whether Confucianism is a religion has long been debated; its ritual side was certainly religious in practice.
