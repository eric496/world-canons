# Tiantai/Tendai and Huayan/Kegon (天台・華嚴)

*6th – 9th century*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

The first schools of Buddhist thought created in China. Tiantai, founded by Zhiyi on Mount Tiantai, ranked all the Buddha's teachings with the Lotus Sutra at the summit and joined doctrine to meditation; Saichō carried it to Japan as Tendai and Uicheon to Korea as Cheontae. Huayan, systematised by Fazang, built on the Flower Garland Sutra a vision of all phenomena interpenetrating; it became Hwaeom in Korea and Kegon in Japan.
