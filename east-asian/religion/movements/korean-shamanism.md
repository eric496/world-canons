# Korean shamanism (muism) (무속 (巫俗))

*ancient – present*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Korean shamanism centres on the mudang, usually a woman, who in a ritual called gut sings, dances and is possessed by gods and ancestors in order to heal, bless, settle the dead and avert misfortune. Its pantheon includes mountain gods, the Seven Stars and the shaman ancestress Princess Bari. Mentioned in Goryeo writings and often disparaged by Joseon Confucians and modern reformers, it survives as a living tradition, several of whose rites are now protected as heritage.
