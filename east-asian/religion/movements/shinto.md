# Kami worship and Shinto (神道)

*ancient – present*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Shinto is the worship of kami, the gods and powers of Japan's land, clans and imperial house, at shrines through purification, offerings, prayers and festivals. Court myth was written down in the Kojiki and Nihon Shoki, and shrine rites were codified in the Engishiki. For most of its history kami worship was intertwined with Buddhism (shinbutsu shūgō); medieval priests at Ise and the Yoshida family, and later nativist scholars, worked to define a distinct Shinto. Historians debate how far a unified 'Shinto' existed before the medieval period.
