# Buddhism in Silla and Goryeo (신라·고려 불교)

*6th – 14th century*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Buddhism became the state religion of Silla in the sixth century and of the Goryeo dynasty (918–1392). Wonhyo and Uisang created a Korean Buddhist scholarship of international standing; Uicheon and Jinul tried to reconcile doctrinal study with Seon meditation. Goryeo carved the entire Buddhist canon twice on woodblocks, and its monks produced the earliest surviving book printed with movable metal type. Under Joseon (from 1392) Confucian rulers curtailed the monasteries.
