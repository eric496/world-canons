# Celestial Masters and early religious Daoism (天師道・上清・靈寶)

*AD 142 – 6th century*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

Organised Daoism began in the second century with Zhang Daoling's Way of the Celestial Masters in Sichuan, a community with priests, registers, confession and healing that revered a deified Laozi. In the fourth and fifth centuries new revealed scriptures created the Shangqing (Highest Clarity) and Lingbao (Numinous Treasure) traditions, with meditation on inner gods, alchemy and grand liturgies. Lu Xiujing and Tao Hongjing gathered these into a canon.
