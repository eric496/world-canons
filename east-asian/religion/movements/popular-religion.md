# Chinese popular and sectarian religion (民間信仰)

*Song dynasty – present*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Most Chinese religious life took place outside any single institution: offerings to ancestors, the Kitchen God, local earth gods and city gods; cults of deified heroes such as Guandi and the sea goddess Mazu; spirit mediums, temple festivals and pilgrimages. Morality books (shanshu) taught a ledger of merit and demerit, while lay sects such as Luo Qing's preached salvation by the Eternal Mother in 'precious scrolls' (baojuan). Governments alternately patronised and suppressed these forms.
