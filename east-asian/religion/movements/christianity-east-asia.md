# Christianity in East Asia (東亞基督教)

*635 – present*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

The Church of the East reached Tang China in 635 and left the Xi'an Stele. Catholic missions followed the Portuguese: Francis Xavier came to Japan in 1549 and Matteo Ricci adapted Christianity to Confucian culture in Ming China. Japan banned the faith from 1614, and Hidden Christians (Kakure Kirishitan) kept it in secret for over two centuries. Korean scholars brought Catholicism home from Beijing in 1784 and suffered repeated persecutions; Protestant missions arrived in the nineteenth century.
