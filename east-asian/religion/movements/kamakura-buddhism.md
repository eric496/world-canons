# Kamakura Buddhism (鎌倉仏教)

*1175 – 1333*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

In an age of war and of the belief that the Buddhist teaching had entered its final, degenerate phase (mappō), Japanese reformers founded new schools that offered a single, accessible practice: Hōnen's Pure Land (Jōdo-shū) and Shinran's True Pure Land (Jōdo Shinshū) relied on the nenbutsu, Eisai and Dōgen brought Rinzai and Sōtō Zen from China, and Nichiren taught devotion to the Lotus Sutra. Modern historians stress that the older Nara and Tendai institutions remained dominant for centuries.
