# Chan, Seon and Zen (禪)

*6th century – present*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

The meditation school traces itself to the Indian monk Bodhidharma and claims a mind-to-mind transmission outside the scriptures. Historically it took shape in Tang China around figures such as Huineng and Linji, developed the literature of encounter dialogues and kōans in the Song, and spread to Korea as Seon and to Japan as Zen, where Eisai, Dōgen, Ikkyū and Hakuin are its best-known masters. Much of its traditional history is now read by scholars as later retrospective lineage-building.
