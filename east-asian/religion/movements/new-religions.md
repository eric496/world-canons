# New religions (新宗教)

*1837 – present*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

From the 1830s, in a time of crisis and Western pressure, charismatic founders who reported direct revelation founded movements outside the established institutions: Nakayama Miki's Tenrikyō and other Japanese new religions, Hong Xiuquan's Taiping Heavenly Kingdom in China, Choe Je-u's Donghak (later Cheondogyo) and Sotaesan's Won Buddhism in Korea. Many offered healing, a coming new world and the equality of believers, and several grew into mass organisations.
