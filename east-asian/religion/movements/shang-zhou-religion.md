# Shang and Zhou religion (商周宗教)

*c. 1250–256 BC*

Era: [Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools](../../periods/early-dynasties.md)

The earliest recorded Chinese religion centred on the royal ancestors, a high god called Di and later Heaven (Tian), and on divination by cracking heated bones and shells. Shang kings offered sacrifices, sometimes including human victims, to keep the dead and the spirits favourable. The Zhou dynasty justified its rule by the Mandate of Heaven and elaborated the ancestral rites that later Confucians took as their model.
