# Pure Land (淨土)

*5th century – present*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

Pure Land devotion focuses on Amitābha (Amida), the Buddha of Infinite Light, who vowed to bring all who call on him to rebirth in his Western Paradise, where enlightenment is assured. Huiyuan's society on Mount Lu, the Chinese masters Tanluan and Shandao, the Japanese Genshin, Hōnen, Shinran and Rennyo shaped it into the most widely practised form of East Asian Buddhism, centred on reciting the Buddha's name (nianfo, nenbutsu).
