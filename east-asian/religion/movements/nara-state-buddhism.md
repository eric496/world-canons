# Asuka and Nara Buddhism (飛鳥・奈良仏教)

*538 – 794*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Buddhism entered Japan officially in the sixth century as a gift from the Korean kingdom of Baekje and was promoted by the court as a protector of the state. Prince Shōtoku, the great temples of Nara, the colossal Buddha of Tōdai-ji and a state network of provincial temples made it part of government, while the six Nara schools studied imported doctrine. Popular tales of karmic reward and wandering ascetics show its spread beyond the court.
