# Buddhism's arrival and the translators (佛教東傳)

*1st – 6th century*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

Buddhism reached China along the Silk Road and the sea routes from about the first century AD, carried by Central Asian and Indian monks and merchants. Generations of translators, above all Kumārajīva, turned Indian scriptures into Chinese, while Chinese pilgrims such as Faxian travelled to India for texts. Through China, Buddhism passed to Korea in the fourth century and to Japan in the sixth.
