# Inner alchemy, Quanzhen and the Daoist canon (內丹・全真)

*11th – 17th century*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

From the Song dynasty Daoist self-cultivation turned inward: inner alchemy (neidan) reinterpreted the language of elixirs as the refinement of the body's own energies. Wang Chongyang founded the monastic Quanzhen (Complete Perfection) order in the twelfth century, blending Daoist, Buddhist and Confucian teaching; under Qiu Chuji it won the favour of Chinggis Khan. The Celestial Masters continued as the Zhengyi order, and the Ming court printed the Daoist canon in 1445.
