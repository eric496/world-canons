# Religion and modernity (近代宗教)

*1868 – present*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

Modern states reshaped religion: Meiji Japan forcibly separated kami from buddhas in 1868 and built State Shinto around the emperor until 1945; Republican and Communist China attacked 'superstition'; colonial Korea saw Buddhist reform and Christian growth. Reformers such as Taixu and Han Yong-un remade Buddhism for the modern world, and D. T. Suzuki presented Zen to Western readers.
