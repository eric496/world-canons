# Esoteric Buddhism and Shingon (密教・真言)

*8th century – present*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Esoteric (tantric) Buddhism, brought to Tang China by Indian masters such as Śubhakarasiṃha, Vajrabodhi and Amoghavajra, teaches that buddhahood can be realised through ritual use of body, speech and mind: mudrā, mantra and visualisation of mandalas. Kūkai brought it to Japan as Shingon in 806, and Tendai adopted much of it. Its deities, such as the fierce Fudō Myōō, and its mountain practices fed into Japanese religion at large, including the ascetic Shugendō.
