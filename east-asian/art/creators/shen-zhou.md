## Biography

Shen Zhou belonged to a wealthy, cultured family of the Suzhou region. Like his father and grandfather, he chose not to sit the examinations or seek office, partly to look after his mother, and lived as a gentleman scholar on the family estate, surrounded by friends, books and art. Generous and good-humoured, he painted for anyone who asked and was widely copied and forged even in his lifetime. Wen Zhengming was his pupil.

Shen Zhou studied all the Yuan masters and earlier models but painted with a broad, sturdy, relaxed brush of his own. He extended literati painting to new subjects, including garden scenes, local landmarks and studies of animals and vegetables. His Lofty Mount Lu, painted in 1467 as a birthday gift for his teacher, is in the manner of Wang Meng. Through him, Suzhou became the centre of Ming scholarly painting.

## Key artworks

### Lofty Mount Lu (1467)

[![Lofty Mount Lu](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a0/Lofty_Mt.Lu_by_Shen_Zhou.jpg/1280px-Lofty_Mt.Lu_by_Shen_Zhou.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lofty_Mt.Lu_by_Shen_Zhou.jpg)

<sub>Shen Zhou, Lofty Mount Lu, 1467 Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lofty_Mt.Lu_by_Shen_Zhou.jpg).</sub>

- **Date:** 1467
- **Type:** Hanging scroll, ink and light colour on paper
- **Where to see it:** National Palace Museum, Taipei
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Lofty%20Mount%20Lu%20Shen%20Zhou)

Shen Zhou painted this large scroll, nearly two metres high, to celebrate the seventieth birthday of his teacher Chen Kuan, whose family came from the region of Mount Lu in Jiangxi. Shen had never seen the mountain, and the landscape is an imaginative tribute rather than a view: a great mass of peaks, a waterfall and pines rise above a tiny scholar on the bank. The dense, layered texture strokes are a homage to the Yuan master Wang Meng, while the long poem at the top praises Chen Kuan's character as lofty as the mountain. It is Shen Zhou's most ambitious early work.
