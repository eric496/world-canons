# Gu Hongzhong

*顧閎中*

**c. 910 – c. 980** · Chinese  
Born: Jiangnan · Died: ?  
Movements & schools: [Tang painting & sancai](../movements/tang-art.md)  

> Painter at the Southern Tang court, traditionally credited with The Night Revels of Han Xizai.

## Biography

Gu Hongzhong served as a painter-in-attendance at the court of Li Yu, last ruler of the Southern Tang kingdom in Nanjing, in the tenth century. Almost nothing else is known about his life. According to the traditional story, Li Yu wanted to make the official Han Xizai a minister but had heard that he spent his nights in revelry, so he sent Gu Hongzhong to observe a party and paint it from memory.

The Night Revels of Han Xizai in the Palace Museum, Beijing, the only work associated with Gu Hongzhong, is now generally regarded as a later copy, perhaps of the Song dynasty, of his composition. Its fine line, rich colour and careful observation of costume, furniture and gesture make it one of the most important figure paintings of early China and a vivid record of elite life in the Five Dynasties.

### The Night Revels of Han Xizai (10th-century composition (probably 12th-century copy))

- **Date:** 10th-century composition (probably 12th-century copy)
- **Type:** Handscroll, ink and colour on silk
- **Where to see it:** Palace Museum, Beijing
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=The%20Night%20Revels%20of%20Han%20Xizai%20Gu%20Hongzhong)

The scroll is divided by screens into five scenes: Han Xizai and his guests listening to a pipa player, watching a dancer while Han beats a drum, resting on a couch, listening to flutes, and saying goodbye to guests. Han appears several times, always unsmiling, with his tall hat, and the painter records furniture, musical instruments, costumes and food in exact detail. According to tradition, the Southern Tang ruler Li Yu sent Gu Hongzhong to observe Han's parties because he was considering him for high office, and Han, sensing the danger of politics, deliberately behaved badly. The work is now generally regarded as a later copy. It is a masterpiece of Chinese figure painting.
