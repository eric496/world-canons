## Biography

Yan Zhenqing came from a family of scholars and passed the civil service examination in 734. During the An Lushan rebellion of 755 he organised loyalist resistance in Hebei, and many of his relatives were killed, including his cousin Yan Gaoqing and his nephew Yan Jiming. He served in high office under several emperors, often clashing with powerful ministers. In old age he was sent to negotiate with the rebel general Li Xilie, who held him captive and had him strangled in 785. He was honoured as a model of loyalty.

Yan Zhenqing broke with the elegant manner of Wang Xizhi to create a regular script of broad, muscular strokes and balanced, square structure, long used to teach children to write. His Draft of a Requiem to My Nephew, written in grief and anger with corrections and blots, is valued as a spontaneous outpouring of feeling. Song critics such as Su Shi and Ouyang Xiu saw his writing as the visible expression of his moral character, an idea central to Chinese calligraphy ever since.

## Key artworks

### Draft of a Requiem to My Nephew (Jizhi wengao) (758)

- **Date:** 758
- **Type:** Calligraphy, ink on paper, handscroll
- **Where to see it:** National Palace Museum, Taipei
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Draft%20of%20a%20Requiem%20to%20My%20Nephew%20%28Jizhi%20wengao%29%20Yan%20Zhenqing)

In 756, during the An Lushan rebellion, Yan Zhenqing's cousin Yan Gaoqing and his son Yan Jiming were captured and killed when their city fell after a heroic defence. Two years later Yan Zhenqing recovered his nephew's head and wrote this eulogy. It is a draft, with words crossed out and inserted, and the writing changes as he goes, from controlled characters to rushing, slanting lines as his anger and grief grow. Later critics valued it precisely because it was not meant as a work of art and so shows feeling directly. Collectors and emperors added seals and colophons, and since the eighteenth century it has often been called the second greatest running-script work in China.
