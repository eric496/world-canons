## Biography

Tang Yin was the son of a Suzhou restaurant owner and became famous young for his talent; in 1498 he came first in the provincial examination. The next year, in Beijing, he was accused of having bought the questions for the metropolitan examination. He was imprisoned, stripped of his degree and barred from office. He returned to Suzhou and lived by selling paintings, building a retreat called the Peach Blossom Hermitage, and became a legendary figure of romance and dissipation in later popular fiction.

Tang Yin studied with the professional painter Zhou Chen and also with Shen Zhou, and he combined academic skill with scholarly expression. His works include landscapes in the manner of Li Tang, refined paintings of beautiful women and poems in an elegant hand. He is usually counted with Shen Zhou, Wen Zhengming and Qiu Ying as one of the Four Masters of the Ming.

## Key artworks

### Tao Gu Presenting a Poem (early 16th century)

[![Tao Gu Presenting a Poem](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c8/Tao_Gu_Presents_a_Poem_by_Tang_Yin.JPG/1280px-Tao_Gu_Presents_a_Poem_by_Tang_Yin.JPG)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tao_Gu_Presents_a_Poem_by_Tang_Yin.JPG)

<sub>Tang Yin, Tao Gu Presenting a Poem Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tao_Gu_Presents_a_Poem_by_Tang_Yin.JPG).</sub>

- **Date:** early 16th century
- **Type:** Hanging scroll, ink and colour on silk
- **Where to see it:** National Palace Museum, Taipei
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Tao%20Gu%20Presenting%20a%20Poem%20Tang%20Yin)

According to the story, the Song envoy Tao Gu, visiting the Southern Tang court, behaved with great dignity until the courtesan Qin Ruolan, disguised as a post-house servant, seduced him, and he gave her a poem, which was later used to shame him at a banquet. Tang Yin shows Tao Gu sitting in a garden under banana palms, listening as she plays the pipa beside a candle. The figures are painted in fine lines and soft colour, and the setting of rocks and trees is elegant. Tang Yin's own poem on the painting comments ironically on the scene. The work shows his mix of professional skill and literary wit.
