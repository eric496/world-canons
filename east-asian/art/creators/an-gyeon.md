# An Gyeon

*안견 (安堅)*

**fl. c. 1440 – c. 1470** · Korean  
Born: Jisan, Chungcheong province · Died: ?  
Movements & schools: [Joseon painting and ceramics](../movements/joseon-painting.md)  

> Court painter of early Joseon, painter of the Dream Journey to the Peach Blossom Land (1447), the foundation of Korean landscape painting.

## Biography

An Gyeon served in the Dohwaseo, the Joseon royal bureau of painting, under King Sejong and his successors and rose to an unusually high rank for a painter. He was a protégé of Prince Anpyeong, Sejong's third son, a great calligrapher and collector who owned Chinese paintings by Guo Xi and others. Few facts about his life are documented.

In 1447 Prince Anpyeong described a dream of wandering into the peach blossom paradise of Tao Yuanming's fable, and An Gyeon painted it in three days. The handscroll, with inscriptions by the prince and twenty-one scholars, shows how An Gyeon adapted Guo Xi's style into a distinctly Korean idiom. It left Korea, probably during the Japanese invasions, and is now in Tenri University Library in Japan. His manner, the An Gyeon school, dominated early Joseon landscape.

## Key artworks

### Dream Journey to the Peach Blossom Land (Mongyu dowondo) (1447)

[![Dream Journey to the Peach Blossom Land (Mongyu dowondo)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/93/Dream_Journey_to_the_Peach_Blossom_Land.jpg/1280px-Dream_Journey_to_the_Peach_Blossom_Land.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dream_Journey_to_the_Peach_Blossom_Land.jpg)

<sub>An Gyeon, Dream Journey to the Peach Blossom Land Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dream_Journey_to_the_Peach_Blossom_Land.jpg).</sub>

- **Date:** 1447
- **Type:** Handscroll, ink and colour on silk
- **Where to see it:** Tenri University Library, Tenri, Japan
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Dream%20Journey%20to%20the%20Peach%20Blossom%20Land%20%28Mongyu%20dowondo%29%20An%20Gyeon)

Prince Anpyeong dreamed in 1447 that he wandered with friends through mountains into a hidden valley of peach blossoms, the paradise described by the Chinese poet Tao Yuanming. He asked An Gyeon to paint it, and the painter finished in three days. Unusually, the scroll reads from left to right: from the ordinary world at the lower left, the viewer passes through craggy, twisting mountains in the Guo Xi manner to the peach blossom valley on the right, shown from above. The prince and twenty-one scholars added inscriptions, making the scroll a document of the brilliant court culture of King Sejong's reign. Taken to Japan, probably during the invasions of the 1590s, it is an Important Cultural Property of Japan and has been shown in Korea only briefly.
