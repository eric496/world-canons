# Zhang Zeduan

*張擇端*

**1085 – 1145** · Chinese  
Born: Dongwu (Zhucheng), Shandong · Died: ?  
Movements & schools: [Song academy & literati painting](../movements/song-academy-literati.md)  

> Northern Song painter known for one work, the panoramic handscroll Along the River During the Qingming Festival.

## Biography

Almost everything known about Zhang Zeduan comes from a colophon written in 1186 by Zhang Zhu on his famous scroll. It says that he came from Dongwu, studied in the capital Kaifeng, and later turned to painting, specialising in ruled-line painting (jiehua) of boats, carts, bridges and buildings. He seems to have worked in or near the court of Emperor Huizong. The dates often given for him are not securely documented.

Along the River During the Qingming Festival, now in the Palace Museum, Beijing, shows a riverside city from the countryside to the busy centre, with more than 800 people and accurate details of trade, transport and architecture. It is one of the most famous paintings in China and was endlessly copied and reinterpreted, especially in the Ming and Qing, so that dozens of later versions exist.

## Key artworks

### Along the River During the Qingming Festival (early 12th century)

<sub>Along the River During the Qingming Festival (detail of the city gate) Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bianjing_city_gate.JPG).</sub>

- **Date:** early 12th century
- **Type:** Handscroll, ink and colour on silk
- **Where to see it:** Palace Museum, Beijing
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Along%20the%20River%20During%20the%20Qingming%20Festival%20Zhang%20Zeduan)

Unrolled from right to left, the scroll moves from quiet countryside with farmhouses and willows, along the river with heavily loaded barges, to the climax at a great rainbow bridge where a boat is about to collide with it and crowds watch from above, and then through a city gate into busy streets of shops, inns and temples. Zhang Zeduan recorded carts, boats and buildings with the precision of ruled-line painting, and people at every kind of activity. It is usually thought to show the Northern Song capital Kaifeng, though the exact festival and place are debated. The scroll was copied and reworked many times, and the Qing court version by five painters is also famous. Its rare displays in Beijing draw huge queues.
