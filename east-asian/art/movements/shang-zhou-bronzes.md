# Shang & Zhou ritual bronzes (商周青銅器)

*c. 1600–221 BC*

Era: [Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools](../../periods/early-dynasties.md)

For more than a thousand years the most prestigious art in China was cast bronze: vessels for offering food and wine to the ancestors, bells and weapons. Shang foundries at Anyang used piece-mould casting on a scale unmatched anywhere in the ancient world, covering vessels with the staring taotie mask. Zhou bronzes carry long cast inscriptions that are among the earliest Chinese historical texts, and in Sichuan the Sanxingdui culture made strange bronze heads and figures with no parallel in the Yellow River valley.
