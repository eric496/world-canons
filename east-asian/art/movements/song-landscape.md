# Monumental landscape (Five Dynasties & Northern Song) (北宋山水)

*c. 900–1127*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

In the tenth and eleventh centuries landscape became the highest subject of Chinese painting. Painters such as Dong Yuan, Fan Kuan and Guo Xi built towering mountains from layers of ink texture strokes, with tiny travellers and temples to give scale. These hanging scrolls express a Neo-Confucian sense of the order of nature, and they remained the classical model for every later landscape painter in East Asia.
