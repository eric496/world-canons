# Qing painting: Orthodox masters and Individualists (清代繪畫)

*1644–1912*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

After the Manchu conquest, the Four Wangs of the Orthodox school followed Dong Qichang in synthesising the old masters, and Wang Hui led the court's great documentary scrolls. Loyalist monk-painters such as Bada Shanren and Shitao, descendants of the Ming imperial house, turned to eccentric, expressive styles. At the Qianlong court the Italian Jesuit Giuseppe Castiglione blended European shading with Chinese media, and the Jingdezhen kilns reached new technical heights with famille rose enamels.
