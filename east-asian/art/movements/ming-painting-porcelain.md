# Ming painting & porcelain (明代書畫與瓷器)

*1368–1644*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

Ming painting divided between the professional Zhe school, heir to the Song academy, and the Wu school of scholar-painters in Suzhou led by Shen Zhou and Wen Zhengming, with Tang Yin and Qiu Ying between the two. At the end of the dynasty Dong Qichang's theory of Northern and Southern schools defined the literati canon. The imperial kilns at Jingdezhen made blue-and-white porcelain that was exported across Asia, the Islamic world and Europe, and court workshops produced carved lacquer and cloisonné.
