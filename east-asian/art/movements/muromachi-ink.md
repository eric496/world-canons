# Muromachi ink painting (室町水墨画)

*c. 1350–1573*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

Zen monks brought Chinese ink painting to Japan, and in the monasteries of Kyoto painters such as Josetsu and Shūbun adapted Song and Yuan models. Sesshū, who travelled to Ming China, gave suiboku-ga (water-and-ink painting) a forceful Japanese voice. Ink painting became bound up with Zen practice, the tea ceremony and the shoin reception room.
