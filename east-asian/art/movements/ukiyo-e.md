# Ukiyo-e (浮世絵)

*c. 1670–1900*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Pictures of the floating world: woodblock prints and paintings of the actors, courtesans, beauties and pleasures of Edo's licensed quarters and kabuki theatres. Polychrome brocade prints (nishiki-e) appeared in 1765 with Suzuki Harunobu. Utamaro and Sharaku took the beauty and actor portrait to its height in the 1790s, and in the nineteenth century Hokusai and Hiroshige made the landscape print a mass-market sensation. Exported to Europe, ukiyo-e inspired Japonisme and the Impressionists.
