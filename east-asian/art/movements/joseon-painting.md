# Joseon painting and ceramics (조선 회화)

*1392–1910*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Early Joseon painters such as An Gyeon worked in Chinese landscape modes, but in the eighteenth century Jeong Seon began painting real Korean mountains in true-view (jingyeong) landscapes, and Kim Hong-do and Shin Yun-bok painted the everyday life of commoners and the pleasures of the elite. Kim Jeong-hui led a late scholarly revival. Joseon potters made buncheong ware and austere white porcelain, including the moon jars.
