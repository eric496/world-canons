# Tang painting & sancai (唐代繪畫與三彩)

*618–907*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

The Tang capital Chang'an was the largest city in the world and its art was confident and cosmopolitan. Court painters such as Yan Liben, Han Gan and Zhou Fang painted emperors, horses and plump court ladies; Wu Daozi was celebrated for his sweeping temple murals, now lost. Tomb potters made figures of camels, horses and foreign merchants in three-colour sancai glazes, and calligraphers such as Yan Zhenqing gave the brush new monumental force.
