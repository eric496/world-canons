# Qin and Han tomb art (秦漢墓葬藝術)

*221 BC – AD 220*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

The first emperors believed the dead needed a whole world below ground. Qin Shi Huang's mausoleum was guarded by thousands of life-size terracotta soldiers, and Han tombs were furnished with jade suits, lacquer, painted silk banners, bronze lamps and pottery models of houses, granaries and animals. Much of what we know of early Chinese painting and sculpture comes from these burials.
