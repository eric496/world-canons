# Postwar avant-gardes (戰後前衛)

*1945–2000*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/east-asian-contemporary.md)

After 1945 East Asian artists joined, and sometimes led, the international avant-garde. In Japan the Gutai group staged performance and action painting from 1954, and Mono-ha in the late 1960s worked with raw materials. Korean artists developed Dansaekhwa monochrome painting, Nam June Paik invented video art, Yayoi Kusama worked in New York, and in post-Mao China artists of the 1985 New Wave such as Xu Bing questioned the written word itself. Ink modernists in Taiwan and Hong Kong reworked the brush for a new age.
