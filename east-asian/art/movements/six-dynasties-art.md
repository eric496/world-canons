# Six Dynasties calligraphy & figure painting (六朝書畫)

*220–589*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

In the centuries of division after the Han, calligraphy and painting became the arts of educated gentlemen. Wang Xizhi set the standard for the brush-written character, Gu Kaizhi painted the earliest figure scrolls to survive in copies, and Xie He wrote the Six Principles of painting, headed by spirit resonance, which critics cited for the next 1,500 years.
