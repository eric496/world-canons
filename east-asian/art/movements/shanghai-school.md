# Shanghai school and modern Chinese ink (海上畫派)

*c. 1850–1950*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

In the treaty port of Shanghai, painters sold to a new merchant public rather than to scholar-officials. Ren Bonian and Wu Changshuo combined bright colour, bold calligraphic brushwork and the archaic flavour of seal carving. Their heirs, above all Qi Baishi, made popular, vigorous ink painting of flowers, shrimps and insects, while Xu Beihong, trained in Paris, sought to reform Chinese painting with Western realism.
