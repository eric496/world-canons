# Edo individualists, Maruyama–Shijō and Nanga (江戸絵画)

*c. 1700–1850*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Eighteenth-century Kyoto was full of independent painters. Itō Jakuchū painted birds and flowers with obsessive, brilliant detail; Maruyama Ōkyo introduced a calm naturalism based on sketching from life; and Ike no Taiga and Yosa Buson created Japanese literati painting (nanga or bunjinga) after Chinese models they knew mostly from books and prints.
