# Song and Goryeo ceramics (宋代陶瓷·고려청자)

*960–1392*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Song potters pursued subtle, monochrome glazes rather than painted decoration: the bluish crackled Ru ware of the imperial court, Jun, Ding and Longquan wares, and the dark Jian tea bowls prized by Japanese tea masters. In Korea, Goryeo potters developed celadon of a famous kingfisher green and the unique inlay technique called sanggam. Vietnamese kilns of the Lý and Trần dynasties made their own celadons and brown-painted wares in dialogue with China.
