# Buddhist sculpture and cave temples (佛教造像)

*c. 400–800*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Buddhism reached China along the Silk Road and passed on to Korea and Japan, bringing with it the image of the Buddha. Rulers and believers carved colossal Buddhas into cliffs at Yungang and Longmen, painted hundreds of caves at Dunhuang, and cast gilt-bronze bodhisattvas in Korea and Japan. Styles travelled fast: a Nara statue can echo a Tang model which in turn echoes Gupta India. Buddhist art also spread south into Vietnam, where Champa and Đại Việt developed their own traditions.
