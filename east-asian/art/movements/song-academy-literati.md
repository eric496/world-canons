# Song academy & literati painting (宋代畫院與文人畫)

*c. 1100–1279*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Emperor Huizong made the court painting academy a demanding school of exact observation, and after the court fled south its painters, above all Ma Yuan and Xia Gui, invented an intimate, misty style with one-cornered compositions. At the same time scholar-officials such as Su Shi argued that painting should express the character of the painter, not imitate appearances, laying the foundation of literati painting. Chan (Zen) monk-painters such as Muqi and Liang Kai worked in swift, splashed ink that was treasured above all in Japan.
