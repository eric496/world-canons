# Wasan: Edo mathematics (和算)

*c. 1620 – 1868*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

In Tokugawa Japan, largely cut off from Europe, a native mathematics grew out of Chinese books and the soroban. Seki Takakazu and his school developed determinants, a form of calculus and elaborate geometry, and amateurs hung problems on wooden tablets, sangaku, in temples and shrines.
