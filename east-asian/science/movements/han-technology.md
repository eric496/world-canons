# Paper & Han technology (漢代技術)

*c. 200 BC – AD 589*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

Han and post-Han China produced paper, the wheelbarrow, the seismoscope, water-powered bellows for iron casting and a long tradition of agricultural manuals. Zhang Heng, Ma Jun and Zu Chongzhi combined mathematics, astronomy and the making of machines.
