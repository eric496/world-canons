# Han mathematics (漢代算學)

*c. 200 BC – AD 300*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

Using counting rods on a board, Han officials and scholars solved practical problems of land, grain, taxes and engineering. The Zhoubi Suanjing and the Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art gathered this knowledge into problem books, and Liu Hui's commentary of 263 added proofs and an algorithm for π.
