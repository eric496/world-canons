# Science since 1945 (現代科學)

*1945 – present*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/east-asian-contemporary.md)

After 1945 East Asian physicists, chemists and physicians joined the front rank of world science, winning Nobel prizes from Yukawa's in 1949 onward, while Japan, South Korea and China became leading industrial and technological powers.
