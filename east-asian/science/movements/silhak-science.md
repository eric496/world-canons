# Silhak and late Joseon science (實學)

*1700 – 1900*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Korea's practical-learning scholars, many of whom travelled to Beijing, took up Western astronomy and mathematics, argued for a rotating earth, and turned to mapping and medicine; Heo Jun's medical encyclopedia and Kim Jeong-ho's great map bracket the period.
