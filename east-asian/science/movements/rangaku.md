# Rangaku: Dutch learning (蘭學)

*1720 – 1868*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Through the Dutch trading post at Dejima in Nagasaki, Japanese physicians, interpreters and scholars read European books on anatomy, medicine, astronomy and physics. The translation of a Dutch anatomy text in 1774 launched the movement, which went on to surgery, vaccination, electricity and modern surveying.
