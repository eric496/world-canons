# Gunpowder & the compass (火藥與指南針)

*c. 850 – 1400*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Gunpowder formulas first appear in print in 1044 and fire lances, bombs and early guns followed in the wars of Song, Jin and Mongols. The magnetized needle is described by Shen Kuo and was in use for navigation by about 1100. Both spread west across Eurasia in the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries.
