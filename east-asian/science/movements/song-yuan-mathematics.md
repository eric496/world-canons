# Song–Yuan mathematics (宋元數學)

*c. 1050 – 1300*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

A brief golden age of Chinese algebra: Qin Jiushao, Li Ye, Yang Hui and Zhu Shijie solved polynomial equations of high degree, worked with what Europe would call Pascal's triangle and the Chinese remainder theorem, and wrote equations in a positional notation on the counting board. Guo Shoujing's Shoushi calendar applied this skill to astronomy.
