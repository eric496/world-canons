# Classical Chinese medicine (中醫經典)

*c. 200 BC – AD 300*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

The Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic set out a medicine of yin and yang, the five phases, qi and the channels of the body, read through the pulse and treated with acupuncture and drugs. Zhang Zhongjing's Treatise on Cold Damage and the Divine Farmer's materia medica completed the classical canon on which East Asian medicine still builds.
