# Chinese alchemy & pharmacology (煉丹術與本草)

*c. 300 – 1600*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Daoist alchemists searched for elixirs of immortality by heating mercury, sulphur, lead and saltpetre, and in the process learned much practical chemistry; one of their mixtures became gunpowder. The parallel tradition of bencao, the materia medica, classified thousands of drugs and culminated in Li Shizhen's Compendium of 1578.
