# Early astronomy & calendars (天文曆法)

*c. 1300 BC – AD 220*

Era: [Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools](../../periods/early-dynasties.md)

Shang diviners recorded eclipses, novae and the cycle of days on oracle bones, and Zhou and Han court astronomers turned sky-watching into a state service. Because the emperor ruled by the mandate of Heaven, an accurate calendar and the reading of celestial omens were matters of government, and China kept the longest continuous record of comets, novae and sunspots in the world.
