# Meiji and modern Japanese science (近代科學)

*1868 – 1945*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

Meiji Japan built universities, laboratories and institutes on Western models and within a generation produced researchers of world rank in bacteriology, chemistry and physics. China and Korea followed more slowly, amid revolution and colonial rule.
