# Joseon science under Sejong (朝鮮科學)

*1400 – 1600*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

Under King Sejong (r. 1418–50) the Joseon court created a royal observatory, water clocks, sundials and the world's first standardised rain gauges, compiled a Korean calendar and native pharmacopoeias, and invented the Hangul alphabet. The engineer Jang Yeong-sil, born a government slave, built many of the instruments.
