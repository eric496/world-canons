# Jesuit science in China (西學東漸)

*1582 – 1800*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

From Matteo Ricci's arrival in 1582 the Jesuits won a place at the Ming and Qing courts through mathematics, astronomy and maps. With converts such as Xu Guangqi they translated Euclid and reformed the calendar, and Chinese scholars like Mei Wending absorbed the new learning into their own tradition.
