# Ming technology & natural history (明代科技)

*1368 – 1644*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

Ming China produced great compendia of practical knowledge: Li Shizhen's materia medica, Song Yingxing's illustrated survey of industry, Xu Guangqi's agricultural encyclopedia and Zhu Zaiyu's exact equal temperament.
