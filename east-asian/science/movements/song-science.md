# Song science & engineering (宋代科技)

*960 – 1279*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Song China was perhaps the most technically advanced society of its time: Shen Kuo wrote on magnetism, fossils and optics, Su Song built a water-driven astronomical clock tower, and printed manuals spread knowledge of agriculture, medicine and forensic examination.
