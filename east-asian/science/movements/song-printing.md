# Printing in Song, Goryeo & Japan (印刷術)

*c. 700 – 1400*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Woodblock printing, used in Tang China, Silla Korea and Nara Japan from the eighth century, became under the Song a mass medium for classics, medical books and examination guides; the Diamond Sutra of 868 and Korea's Jikji of 1377 (both in the religion section) are its landmarks. Bi Sheng's movable type of the 1040s and Goryeo's cast-metal type followed.
