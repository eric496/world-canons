# Sui–Tang science & medicine (隋唐科技)

*589 – 960*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

The Tang state ran an imperial medical bureau, an astronomical office and a cosmopolitan capital open to Indian and Persian learning. Sun Simiao wrote great medical encyclopedias, the monk Yi Xing measured the meridian and built a water-driven armillary, and woodblock printing spread Buddhist texts and calendars.
