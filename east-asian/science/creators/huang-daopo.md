# Huang Daopo

*黃道婆*

**c. 1245 – c. 1330** · Chinese  
Born: Wunijing, Songjiang (Shanghai) · Died: Wunijing, Songjiang (Shanghai)  
Eras & fields: [Song science & engineering](../movements/song-science.md)  

> Textile innovator who, according to tradition, brought cotton-working techniques from Hainan to the Yangzi delta and made Songjiang the centre of Chinese cotton weaving.

## Biography

The life of Huang Daopo is known from a few Yuan and Ming writers and much local legend. She is said to have fled an unhappy household as a girl and lived for decades on Hainan Island among the Li people, returning to Songjiang near modern Shanghai around 1295.

She taught local people better ginning, bowing, spinning and weaving methods, including a treadle-operated multi-spindle spinning wheel and techniques for patterned cloth. Songjiang became famous for cotton, and she was worshipped as a goddess of the craft. How much is tradition is debated, but she is a rare named woman in the Chinese history of technology.

## Key works & inventions

### Songjiang cotton technology (c. 1295)

[![Songjiang cotton technology](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/91/Huang_Daopo_machine.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Huang_Daopo_machine.jpg)

<sub>Treadle cotton-spinning machine of the kind associated with Huang Daopo Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Huang_Daopo_machine.jpg).</sub>

- **Date:** c. 1295
- **Type:** Textile technology
- **Published / where:** Songjiang (Shanghai), Yuan China
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Songjiang%20cotton%20technology%20Huang%20Daopo)

Cotton reached China from South and Southeast Asia and was grown in the south long before it became common in the Yangzi valley in the thirteenth century. According to the Yuan writer Tao Zongyi, the women of Songjiang had only crude methods until Huang Daopo returned from Hainan and taught them how to remove seeds with a roller gin, loosen the fibre with a bow, spin with a treadle wheel carrying three spindles, and weave patterned cloth. Within a century Songjiang cloth was famous throughout China, and cotton replaced hemp as the main clothing fibre of ordinary people. How much is owed to one woman is uncertain, but her story records a real transfer of technique from the Li people of Hainan.
