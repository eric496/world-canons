## Biography

Hua Tuo appears in the official histories of the Three Kingdoms and the Later Han as a wandering physician of extraordinary skill. He is said to have been executed by the warlord Cao Cao, whom he had treated for headaches, after refusing to stay as his personal doctor.

The histories credit him with abdominal surgery performed after the patient drank mafeisan, an anaesthetic powder taken with wine, whose formula is lost, and with the 'five animals' exercises modelled on the tiger, deer, bear, ape and bird. How much of this is history is debated, but he became the patron of Chinese surgeons, and Hanaoka Seishū in Japan set out to recreate his anaesthetic.
