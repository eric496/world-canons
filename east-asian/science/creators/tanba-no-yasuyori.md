# Tanba no Yasuyori

*丹波康頼*

**912 – 995** · Japanese  
Born: Tanba province · Died: Heian-kyō (Kyoto)  
Eras & fields: [Sui–Tang science & medicine](../movements/sui-tang-science.md)  

> Heian court physician whose Ishinpō (984) is the oldest surviving Japanese medical book, preserving many Chinese texts otherwise lost.

## Biography

Tanba no Yasuyori, said to be descended from Chinese immigrants, served as acupuncture doctor at the Heian court and was rewarded with the family name Tanba. His descendants remained hereditary court physicians for centuries.

His Ishinpō, presented to Emperor En'yū in 984, compiles extracts from over a hundred Chinese medical works of the Sui and Tang in thirty scrolls. Because many of those sources perished in China, it is a key witness to early Chinese medicine, as well as the foundation of Japanese medical writing.

## Key works & inventions

### Ishinpō (984)

[![Ishinpō](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4d/Isinnhou.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Isinnhou.jpg)

<sub>Page of the Ishinpō Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Isinnhou.jpg).</sub>

- **Date:** 984
- **Type:** Medical compendium
- **Published / where:** Heian-kyō (Kyoto), Japan; Nakarai family manuscript in the Tokyo National Museum
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Ishinp%C5%8D%20Tanba%20no%20Yasuyori)

Tanba no Yasuyori compiled the Ishinpō, 'Essential Methods of Medicine', from extracts of more than a hundred Chinese medical texts of the Six Dynasties, Sui and Tang, arranged by subject: general principles, acupuncture, diseases of each part of the body, women's and children's medicine, pharmacy, diet, longevity and the bedchamber. Each passage is cited by source, and since many of these works were later lost in China, the Ishinpō is a primary witness to early Chinese medicine. The finest manuscript, handed down in the Nakarai family of physicians, was bought by the Japanese state in 1982 and designated a National Treasure.
