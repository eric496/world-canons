# Yang Hui

*楊輝*

**c. 1238 – c. 1298** · Chinese  
Born: Qiantang (Hangzhou) · Died: China  
Eras & fields: [Song–Yuan mathematics](../movements/song-yuan-mathematics.md)  

> Southern Song mathematician and teacher whose books preserve the triangle of binomial coefficients, attributed to Jia Xian, and early magic squares.

## Biography

Yang Hui was a minor official in Hangzhou who wrote several mathematical books between 1261 and 1275, aimed in part at teaching. Little else is known about him.

His Detailed Analysis of the Nine Chapters (1261) reproduces the triangular array of binomial coefficients, which he credits to the eleventh-century mathematician Jia Xian; in China it is still called Yang Hui's triangle, in Europe Pascal's. His later books contain magic squares up to order ten and practical arithmetic for merchants and officials.

## Key works & inventions

### Yang Hui's triangle (1261)

[![Yang Hui's triangle](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ea/Yanghui_triangle.gif)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yanghui_triangle.gif)

<sub>The arithmetical triangle in Yang Hui's book Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yanghui_triangle.gif).</sub>

- **Date:** 1261
- **Type:** Mathematical diagram
- **Published / where:** Hangzhou, Southern Song China
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Yang%20Hui%27s%20triangle%20Yang%20Hui)

In his Xiangjie Jiuzhang Suanfa of 1261, Yang Hui reproduced a diagram he attributed to the eleventh-century mathematician Jia Xian, showing the coefficients for expanding (a + b) to the sixth power in a triangle in which each number is the sum of the two above it. Jia Xian used it to extract roots of high order. The same triangle had appeared in India and the Islamic world, notably in the work of al-Karaji and al-Samaw'al, and was later treated by Blaise Pascal in 1654. In China it is called the Yang Hui or Jia Xian triangle, and Zhu Shijie printed it to the eighth power in 1303.
