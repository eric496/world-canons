# Vernacular houses & villages (民居)

*15th – 19th century*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Beyond palaces and temples, farmers and clans built houses adapted to climate and community: the fortified earthen tulou of the Hakka in Fujian, the steep thatched gasshō farmhouses of the Japanese Alps and the tiled and thatched yangban villages of Korea. They are now among the most visited heritage sites of the region.
