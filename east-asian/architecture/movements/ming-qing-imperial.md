# Ming–Qing imperial architecture (明清宮廷建築)

*1368 – 1912*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

The Ming and Qing courts built on a scale never seen before: the Forbidden City, the Temple of Heaven, imperial tombs and vast garden palaces, all governed by strict hierarchies of colour, roof form and number. Construction was run by official bureaus and hereditary families of designers such as the Lei. The Qing also patronised Tibetan Buddhist architecture, whose own great monument, the Potala in Lhasa, belongs to a separate tradition.
