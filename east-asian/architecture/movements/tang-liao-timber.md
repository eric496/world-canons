# Tang & Liao timber halls (唐遼木構建築)

*7th – 11th century*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

The Tang dynasty perfected the Chinese timber frame: columns carrying clusters of interlocking brackets (dougong) that support deep, gently curving eaves. Only a handful of Tang halls survive, in remote mountain temples, but the Liao dynasty in the north continued the style, and the system spread to Korea and Japan, where Tang forms are preserved better than in China itself.
