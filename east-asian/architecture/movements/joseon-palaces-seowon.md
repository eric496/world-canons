# Joseon palaces & seowon (조선 궁궐·서원)

*1392 – 1897*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

The Joseon dynasty laid out its capital at Hanyang (Seoul) according to Confucian ritual and geomancy, with palaces set against mountains, the ancestral shrine of Jongmyo and city walls. Its architecture is restrained, with painted dancheong brackets, and closely fitted to terrain. In the countryside, Confucian academies (seowon) and gentry gardens expressed the scholarly ideals of Neo-Confucianism.
