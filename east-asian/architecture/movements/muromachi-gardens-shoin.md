# Muromachi gardens & shoin (室町庭園・書院造)

*14th – 16th century*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

Zen monks and the Ashikaga shōguns created the classic Japanese garden: the strolling pond garden, the dry landscape (karesansui) of raked gravel and stone, and the garden meant to be viewed from a veranda. Indoors, the shoin study with its alcove, staggered shelves and built-in desk became the model for the formal Japanese room.
