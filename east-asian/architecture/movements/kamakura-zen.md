# Kamakura Buddhist & Zen architecture (鎌倉仏教・禅宗様建築)

*late 12th – 14th century*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

After the civil wars of the 1180s Japanese builders turned again to China. The monk Chōgen rebuilt Tōdai-ji in the bold 'Great Buddha style' from southern China, and Zen monasteries introduced the 'Zen style' of Song China, with curved window heads, dense bracketing and stone-floored halls laid out in a strict axial plan.
