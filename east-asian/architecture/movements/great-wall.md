# The Great Wall (長城)

*7th century BC – 17th century*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

Walls of rammed earth, rubble and later brick and stone were built by rival states of the Warring States period, joined and extended by the Qin and Han, and rebuilt on a vast scale by the Ming. The Great Wall is not one structure but a system of walls, watchtowers, passes and garrisons stretched along the northern frontier over two thousand years.
