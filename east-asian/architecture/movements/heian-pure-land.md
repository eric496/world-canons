# Heian Pure Land halls & shinden-zukuri (平安浄土建築・寝殿造)

*9th – 12th century*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

In Heian Japan the court aristocracy lived in shinden-zukuri mansions, pavilions linked by corridors around a pond garden. The same idea, applied to Pure Land Buddhism, produced Amida halls set over water to evoke the Western Paradise, most famously the Phoenix Hall of Byōdō-in. Construction became lighter, more Japanese and more closely bound to landscape.
