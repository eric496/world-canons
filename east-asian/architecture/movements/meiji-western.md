# Meiji Western-style & early modern (明治洋風建築・近代建築)

*1868 – 1945*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

After 1868 the Meiji government hired foreign architects such as Josiah Conder to teach Western building, and his Japanese students produced brick banks, stations and palaces in European styles. Frank Lloyd Wright and Antonin Raymond brought modern ideas, while in China architects trained abroad sought a modern national style, as in the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum.
