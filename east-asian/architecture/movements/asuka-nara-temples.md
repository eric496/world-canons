# Asuka & Nara temple architecture (飛鳥・奈良寺院建築)

*late 6th – 8th century*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Buddhism came to Japan from Baekje in the sixth century, and with it Korean and Chinese carpenters, roof-tile makers and painters. Temple compounds with a pagoda, a golden hall and cloisters were built at Asuka and then at Nara, the capital laid out on the Chinese grid. They include the oldest surviving wooden buildings in the world, at Hōryū-ji.
