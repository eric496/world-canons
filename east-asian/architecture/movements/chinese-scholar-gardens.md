# Chinese scholar gardens (江南文人園林)

*16th – 19th century*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

In the prosperous cities of the lower Yangzi, above all Suzhou, retired officials and merchants built walled gardens of rockeries, ponds, pavilions and covered walkways. Designed to unfold as a sequence of framed views, like a handscroll painting, they compress a whole landscape into a city plot. Ji Cheng's treatise Yuanye of 1631 set out their principles.
