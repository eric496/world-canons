# Japanese modernism & Metabolism (モダニズム・メタボリズム)

*1945 – 1980s*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/east-asian-contemporary.md)

Postwar reconstruction made Japan a centre of modern architecture. Kunio Maekawa and Kenzō Tange fused Le Corbusier's concrete with Japanese structural clarity, and in 1960 younger architects launched Metabolism, a vision of megastructures and replaceable capsules that would grow like organisms. In Korea Kim Swoo-geun and Kim Chung-up created a comparable modern architecture.
