# Early Chinese palaces & tombs (秦漢宮殿陵墓)

*c. 1600 BC – AD 220*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

From the rammed-earth platforms of the Shang to the vast palace compounds of Qin and Han, early Chinese builders worked in pounded earth and timber, setting halls on raised terraces along a north–south axis. Little survives above ground, so these buildings are known from excavated foundations, clay house models and above all from tombs, which were laid out as underground palaces for the dead.
