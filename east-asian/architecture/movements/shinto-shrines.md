# Shinto shrine architecture (神社建築)

*7th century – present*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

Shinto shrines preserve forms older than Buddhist architecture in Japan: raised floors, thatched or bark roofs, unpainted timber and crossed finials. Ise and Izumo represent the two most ancient types, kept alive by periodic rebuilding rather than by preserving old fabric, while later shrines such as Itsukushima absorbed the colour and planning of Buddhist and palace architecture.
