# Momoyama & early Edo castles and palaces (桃山・江戸初期城郭)

*1570s – 1650s*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

The wars of unification produced the Japanese castle: a many-storeyed keep (tenshu) of plastered timber on a high stone base, surrounded by moats and baileys. Their audience halls, painted with gold-ground screens, and the shrine-mausolea of the Tokugawa at Nikkō represent the most opulent phase of Japanese architecture.
