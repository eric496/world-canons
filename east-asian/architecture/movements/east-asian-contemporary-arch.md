# Contemporary architecture (現代建築)

*1980s – present*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/east-asian-contemporary.md)

Since the 1980s East Asian architects have become leading figures worldwide, winning the Pritzker Prize repeatedly. Tadao Andō's concrete, Toyo Itō's and SANAA's lightness, Kengo Kuma's timber and Wang Shu's recycled tiles all reinterpret local tradition, while China's building boom has produced some of the largest projects on earth.
