# Three Kingdoms, Silla & Goryeo Buddhist architecture (삼국·신라·고려 불교건축)

*4th – 14th century*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

The Korean kingdoms of Goguryeo, Baekje and Silla adopted Buddhism in the fourth to sixth centuries and became the bridge that carried Chinese building to Japan. Their great timber temples are lost, but stone pagodas, the granite grotto of Seokguram and the terraces of Bulguksa show a distinctive refinement, and a few Goryeo timber halls are the oldest wooden buildings in Korea.
