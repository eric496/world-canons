# Sukiya & the tea house (数寄屋・茶室)

*late 16th – 17th century*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

Against Momoyama splendour, tea masters such as Sen no Rikyū developed the tiny rustic tea room of earth walls, bamboo and paper, entered through a low door. The sukiya style that grew from it, with natural materials, asymmetry and understated refinement, shaped villas such as Katsura and remains a living ideal of Japanese domestic architecture.
