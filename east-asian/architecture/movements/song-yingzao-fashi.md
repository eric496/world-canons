# Song architecture & the Yingzao Fashi (宋代建築・營造法式)

*960 – 1279*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

Song dynasty architecture became more elaborate and more standardised. In 1103 the court published the Yingzao Fashi, a building manual that fixed a modular system of timber sizes, bracket sets and labour quotas. Surviving Song halls and pagodas show slenderer proportions, richer bracketing and the beginnings of the decorative intensity of later imperial architecture.
