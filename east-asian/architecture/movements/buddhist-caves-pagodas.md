# Buddhist cave temples & early pagodas (石窟寺與佛塔)

*4th – 9th century*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

Buddhism reached China along the Silk Road, bringing the rock-cut sanctuary and the stupa. Chinese builders carved cave temples into cliffs at Dunhuang, Yungang and Longmen and transformed the Indian stupa into the many-storeyed pagoda, first in timber and then in brick and stone, which became one of the defining forms of East Asian architecture.
