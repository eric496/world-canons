# Liang Shuming

*梁漱溟*

**1893 – 1988** · Chinese  
Born: Beijing · Died: Beijing  
Schools & eras: [New Confucianism](../movements/new-confucianism.md)  

> Philosopher and rural reformer who defended Confucian culture against Westernisation and is counted the first New Confucian.

## Biography

Liang Shuming, born into a Mongol-descended official family in Beijing, was largely self-educated. A youthful Buddhist, he was invited to teach Indian philosophy at Peking University in 1917. In the 1930s he led a rural reconstruction movement in Shandong and later helped found the Democratic League. After 1949 he stayed in the People's Republic, and in 1953 publicly confronted Mao Zedong over the treatment of peasants, which ended his public career; he remained in Beijing and lived to see a revival of interest in his work.

His Eastern and Western Cultures and Their Philosophies (1921) argued that Western, Chinese and Indian cultures embody three different attitudes of the will, forward striving, harmony and renunciation, and that the world would move from the first to the Confucian attitude. It began the modern defence of Confucianism as a living philosophy.

## Key key texts

### Eastern and Western Cultures and Their Philosophies (Dong-Xi wenhua ji qi zhexue) (1921)

- **Date:** 1921
- **Type:** Philosophy of culture
- **Written / first published:** Lectures in Beijing and Jinan; published in Beijing
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Eastern%20and%20Western%20Cultures%20and%20Their%20Philosophies%20%28Dong-Xi%20wenhua%20ji%20qi%20zhexue%29%20Liang%20Shuming)

Delivered as lectures in 1920–21 and published in 1921, at the height of the May Fourth attack on tradition, this book argued that cultures differ in their basic orientation of the will: the West presses forward to master nature, China seeks harmony and balance, and India turns back to renounce desire. Each suits a stage of human development, and the world, having reached material sufficiency through Western science, would next need the Confucian attitude, with India's to come later. Drawing on Bergson and Yogācāra Buddhism as well as Confucius and Wang Yangming, it became a bestseller and launched the cultural conservatism later called New Confucianism.
