# Zou Yan

*鄒衍*

**c. 305 BC – c. 240 BC** · Chinese  
Born: State of Qi · Died: ?  
Schools & eras: [Yin-Yang & Han Cosmology](../movements/yin-yang-han.md)  

> Scholar of Qi regarded as the founder of the Yin-Yang school, who linked the Five Phases to the rise and fall of dynasties.

## Biography

Zou Yan was a member of the Jixia academy in Qi in the third century BC and, according to Sima Qian, was received with great honour at several courts. None of his writings, said to have run to more than a hundred thousand words, survive; we know him through summaries by Sima Qian and other Han authors.

He is credited with systematising the theory of the Five Phases (wood, fire, earth, metal, water) and yin and yang into a cosmic cycle in which each dynasty rules by the virtue of one phase and is overcome by the next. He also imagined China as only one of eighty-one continents. His correlative cosmology shaped the self-image of the Qin and Han empires and was absorbed into Han Confucianism, medicine and divination.
