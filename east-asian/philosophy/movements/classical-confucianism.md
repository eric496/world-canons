# Classical Confucianism (儒家)

*c. 500–230 BC*

Era: [Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools](../../periods/early-dynasties.md)

The ru, or classicists, who looked back to the early Zhou kings as models of humane government. Confucius taught that a good society rests on cultivated persons who practise benevolence (ren) and ritual propriety (li); Mencius argued that human nature tends toward goodness, Xunzi that goodness must be built by ritual and learning. Their texts became the core of education across East Asia for two millennia.
