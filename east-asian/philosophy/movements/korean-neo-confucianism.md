# Korean Neo-Confucianism (性理學)

*c. 1390–1600*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

The Joseon dynasty made Zhu Xi's teaching its state ideology, and Korean scholars pushed its problems further than anyone in China. Jeong Do-jeon's attack on Buddhism laid the foundation; Yi Hwang (Toegye) and Yi I (Yulgok), in the Four–Seven debate and beyond, argued over how principle and vital force relate in human emotions.
