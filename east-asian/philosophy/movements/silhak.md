# Silhak (Practical Learning) (實學)

*c. 1650–1850*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Korean reformers of the late Joseon who turned Confucian learning toward land reform, administration, technology and commerce, some looking to Qing China and Western learning for models. Jeong Yak-yong (Dasan) produced its greatest synthesis, combining classical scholarship with detailed plans for just government.

## Philosophers

-  [Bak Ji-won](../creators/bak-ji-won.md) (1737 – 1805)
-  [Jeong Yak-yong](../creators/jeong-yak-yong.md) (1762 – 1836)
