# Kyoto School (京都学派)

*c. 1910–1990*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

Philosophers centred on Kyoto Imperial University who joined Western philosophy to Buddhist ideas of nothingness. Nishida Kitarō's pure experience and logic of place, Tanabe Hajime's metanoetics, Watsuji Tetsurō's ethics of betweenness and Nishitani Keiji's analysis of nihilism form the first major non-Western philosophy in conversation with Europe. Its wartime politics remain debated.

## Philosophers

-  [Nishida Kitarō](../creators/nishida-kitaro.md) (1870 – 1945)
