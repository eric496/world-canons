# Neo-Confucianism: Lu–Wang School of Mind (陸王心學)

*c. 1170–1600*

Era: [Ming, Muromachi & Early Joseon](../../periods/ming-muromachi-joseon.md)

The rival wing of Neo-Confucianism that located principle in the mind itself. Lu Jiuyuan debated Zhu Xi on this point in 1175; Wang Yangming in the Ming taught innate knowing of the good and the unity of knowing and acting. His followers, including the iconoclast Li Zhi, carried the idea toward a celebration of spontaneous feeling.
