# New Confucianism (現代新儒家)

*c. 1920–1995*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

Twentieth-century philosophers who defended Confucian thought against May Fourth iconoclasm and Marxism by restating it in dialogue with Kant, Hegel and Buddhism. Liang Shuming, Xiong Shili and Feng Youlan worked in mainland China; Mou Zongsan and others carried the project to Hong Kong and Taiwan.

## Philosophers

-  [Xiong Shili](../creators/xiong-shili.md) (1885 – 1968)
-  [Liang Shuming](../creators/liang-shuming.md) (1893 – 1988)
-  [Feng Youlan](../creators/feng-youlan.md) (1895 – 1990)
-  [Mou Zongsan](../creators/mou-zongsan.md) (1909 – 1995)
