# Ming–Qing Critique & Evidential Learning (考證學)

*c. 1640–1800*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

After the Manchu conquest, loyalist scholars Huang Zongxi, Gu Yanwu and Wang Fuzhi blamed Ming collapse on empty speculation and turned to institutions, history and concrete things. In the eighteenth century the kaozheng movement applied philology, phonology and textual criticism to the classics; Dai Zhen used these methods to attack the Neo-Confucian split between principle and desire.
