# Classical Daoism (道家)

*c. 400–200 BC*

Era: [Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools](../../periods/early-dynasties.md)

A loose label, applied by Han historians, for the texts attached to Laozi and Zhuangzi. They describe a nameless Dao, the way things spontaneously go, and recommend non-coercive action (wuwei), simplicity and a sceptical distance from fixed values and distinctions. The Daodejing addresses rulers; the Zhuangzi uses stories, jokes and paradoxes to loosen the grip of ordinary perspectives.
