# Yin-Yang & Han Cosmology (陰陽家・漢代經學)

*c. 300 BC – AD 220*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

Correlative thinking that maps the cosmos, the seasons, the body and the state onto the complementary forces of yin and yang and the Five Phases. Begun by Zou Yan and the commentators of the Book of Changes, it was woven into Confucian political theory by Dong Zhongshu when Han emperors made the Confucian classics state orthodoxy. Wang Chong's Lunheng is its great sceptical critic.
