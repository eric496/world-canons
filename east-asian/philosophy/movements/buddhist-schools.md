# Buddhist Scholastic Schools (天台・華嚴・密教)

*c. 550–1250*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

The great East Asian systematisations of Buddhist thought. Tiantai (Tendai) and Huayan (Hwaeom, Kegon) ranked the sutras into graded teachings and developed a metaphysics of mutual interpenetration, in which every phenomenon contains every other. In Korea Wonhyo and Uisang, and in Japan Prince Shōtoku, Kūkai and Shinran, made these and the esoteric and Pure Land traditions their own.
