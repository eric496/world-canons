# Kokugaku (National Learning) (國學)

*c. 1690–1850*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

A Japanese movement that studied the oldest native texts, the Kojiki, the Man'yōshū and The Tale of Genji, to recover a Japanese mind before Chinese influence. Motoori Norinaga found it in sincere feeling and the sensitivity he called mono no aware; the movement fed later Shinto nationalism.
