# School of Names (名家)

*c. 380–250 BC*

Era: [Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools](../../periods/early-dynasties.md)

Debaters (bianzhe) such as Hui Shi and Gongsun Long who examined how names relate to things, and propounded paradoxes about space, time, similarity and difference, most famously that a white horse is not a horse. Little of their work survives, mostly through the reports of critics, but they are the closest Chinese counterpart to the Greek sophists and logicians.
