# Legalism (法家)

*c. 350–200 BC*

Era: [Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools](../../periods/early-dynasties.md)

Statecraft theorists, from Shang Yang to Han Fei, who held that order comes from clear published laws, predictable rewards and punishments, and the ruler's control of bureaucratic technique, not from moral example. Their methods helped Qin unify China in 221 BC; the dynasty's swift collapse gave the label a bad name, though Legalist administration survived beneath a Confucian surface.
