# Reform & Enlightenment (變法・啓蒙)

*c. 1860–1945*

Era: [Revolution & Modernisation](../../periods/east-asian-modern.md)

Thinkers who confronted Western power and ideas. In Japan Fukuzawa Yukichi preached independence and civilisation; in China Kang Youwei recast Confucius as a reformer, Liang Qichao called for a new citizen and Hu Shi for pragmatism and science; in Korea Choe Je-u founded Donghak and Han Yong-un called for Buddhist reform.
