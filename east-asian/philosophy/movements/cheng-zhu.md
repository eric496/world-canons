# Neo-Confucianism: Cheng–Zhu School (程朱理學)

*c. 1000–1200*

Era: [Song, Heian & Goryeo](../../periods/song-heian-goryeo.md)

The Song revival of Confucianism as a complete philosophy answering Buddhism. Zhou Dunyi, Zhang Zai and the Cheng brothers built a metaphysics of principle (li) and vital stuff (qi); Zhu Xi synthesised it, centred learning on the Four Books and the 'investigation of things', and from 1313 his commentaries were the basis of the civil-service examinations, later also in Korea and Japan.
