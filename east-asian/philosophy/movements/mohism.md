# Mohism (墨家)

*c. 450–220 BC*

Era: [Bronze Age & the Hundred Schools](../../periods/early-dynasties.md)

The followers of Mozi, the first organised rivals of the Confucians. They argued for impartial concern for everyone (jian ai), judged doctrines by their benefit to the people, condemned aggressive war and lavish ritual, and built a disciplined community of defensive engineers. Later Mohists wrote sophisticated canons on logic, ethics, optics and mechanics. The school vanished after the Qin unification and was rediscovered only in the Qing.
