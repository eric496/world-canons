# Chan, Seon & Zen (禪)

*c. 650–1300*

Era: [The Cosmopolitan Age](../../periods/cosmopolitan-age.md)

The meditation school that claimed a 'separate transmission outside the scriptures' pointing directly at the mind. Its Platform Sutra debated sudden versus gradual awakening; in Korea Jinul joined sudden awakening to gradual cultivation; in Japan Dōgen wrote a profound philosophy of time, being and practice. Its ideas returned to philosophy proper in the Kyoto School.
