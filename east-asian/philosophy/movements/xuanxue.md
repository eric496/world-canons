# Xuanxue (Learning of the Mysterious) (玄學)

*c. 220–420*

Era: [The First Empires](../../periods/empire-formation.md)

The 'Neo-Daoist' metaphysics of the Wei and Jin, carried on in commentaries and 'pure conversation'. Wang Bi read the Laozi and the Changes as pointing to a formless root, nothingness (wu), beneath all things; Guo Xiang answered that things arise spontaneously from themselves. Their vocabulary shaped how Chinese readers first understood Buddhist emptiness.
