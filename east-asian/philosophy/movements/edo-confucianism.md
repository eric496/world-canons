# Edo Confucianism (江戸儒學)

*c. 1600–1800*

Era: [Qing, Edo & Late Joseon](../../periods/qing-edo-late-joseon.md)

Under the Tokugawa shogunate Confucianism became Japan's leading secular learning. Hayashi Razan promoted Zhu Xi orthodoxy, Nakae Tōju Wang Yangming's learning; Itō Jinsai and Ogyū Sorai rejected Song metaphysics altogether and went back to the ancient texts, Sorai treating the Way as institutions invented by ancient kings. Andō Shōeki attacked the whole system in the name of farmers' labour.
