# The Middle Ages

*c. 500 – 1400*

After the fall of the Western Roman Empire, monasteries, cathedrals and universities carry learning forward. Romanesque and Gothic building, scholastic philosophy, Byzantine and Islamic scholarship, chant and polyphony, Dante and the first vernacular literatures.
