# Baroque & the Scientific Revolution

*c. 1600 – 1700*

Drama and splendour in art, architecture and music; Galileo, Kepler and Newton remake the cosmos; Descartes, Spinoza and Locke remake philosophy; religious wars give way to new ideas of the state.
