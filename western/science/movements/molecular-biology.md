# Genetics & molecular biology

*c. 1940–1965*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

Physicists and chemists turned to the question of how heredity works at the level of molecules. X-ray crystallography revealed the double-helix structure of DNA in 1953, showing how genetic information is stored and copied, and the following decade worked out how that information is turned into proteins.
