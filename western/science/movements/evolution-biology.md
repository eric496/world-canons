# Geology, evolution & heredity

*c. 1830–1900*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Geologists showed the Earth to be immensely old and shaped by slow, ordinary processes. On that timescale Darwin and Wallace explained the diversity of life through natural selection, and Mendel's quiet experiments with peas found the rules of inheritance, overlooked until 1900.
