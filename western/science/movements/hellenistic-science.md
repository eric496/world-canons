# Hellenistic science & mathematics

*c. 300 BC – AD 150*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

After Alexander's conquests, royal patronage in Alexandria, with its Museum and Library, made the city the capital of learning. Euclid set geometry in axiomatic form, Archimedes joined mathematics to mechanics, Eratosthenes measured the Earth and Hipparchus put astronomy on a quantitative footing. The Antikythera mechanism shows how far their technical skill could reach.
