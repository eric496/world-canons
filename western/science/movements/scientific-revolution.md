# Scientific Revolution

*c. 1600–1687*

Era: [Baroque & the Scientific Revolution](../../periods/seventeenth-century.md)

In a few generations the Aristotelian picture of nature gave way to a mechanical, mathematical one. Kepler found the laws of planetary motion, Galileo turned the telescope on the sky and founded a mathematical science of motion, Harvey discovered the circulation of the blood, and the microscope revealed a hidden world. Scientific societies and journals made knowledge a public, shared enterprise.
