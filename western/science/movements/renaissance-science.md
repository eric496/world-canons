# Renaissance anatomy & astronomy

*c. 1450–1600*

Era: [The Renaissance](../../periods/renaissance.md)

Printing, the recovery of ancient texts and a new trust in direct observation transformed the study of the body and the heavens. Leonardo dissected and drew, Vesalius corrected Galen from the dissecting table, Copernicus set the Sun at the centre of the planetary system and Tycho Brahe built instruments of unprecedented precision.
