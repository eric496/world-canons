# Roman & late-antique science

*c. 50 BC – AD 500*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

Under Roman rule Greek remained the language of science. Ptolemy's astronomy and geography and Galen's medicine became the great summaries of ancient knowledge, authoritative for more than a millennium. Late-antique teachers such as Hypatia of Alexandria preserved and commented on these texts as the classical world faded.

## Scientists

-  [Claudius Ptolemy](../creators/ptolemy.md) (c. AD 100 – c. AD 170)
-  [Galen of Pergamon](../creators/galen.md) (AD 129 – c. 216)
-  [Hypatia of Alexandria](../creators/hypatia.md) (c. 355–370 – 415)
