# Greek natural philosophy & medicine

*c. 600–300 BC*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

In the Greek cities of Ionia, southern Italy and Athens, thinkers began to explain the world through natural causes, argument and observation rather than myth. Hippocratic physicians treated disease as a natural process with natural remedies, and Aristotle built a vast system of physics, cosmology and biology that shaped European thought for two thousand years.
