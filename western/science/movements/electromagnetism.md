# Electromagnetism & classical physics

*c. 1820–1900*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Faraday's experiments revealed the intimate link between electricity and magnetism and gave the world the dynamo and the idea of the field. Maxwell expressed the whole subject in a set of equations and showed light to be an electromagnetic wave. Alongside this, the science of heat and energy, thermodynamics, took shape, and the century ended with the discovery of X-rays.
