# Islamic science & the translation movement

*c. 750–1250*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

In Abbasid Baghdad and across the Islamic world, Greek, Persian and Indian works were translated into Arabic and then extended. Scholars developed algebra and Indian decimal numerals, a new experimental optics and a systematic medicine. From the twelfth century, translations from Arabic into Latin in Spain and Sicily carried this learning, and much of Greek science with it, into Europe. This tradition will have its own section.
