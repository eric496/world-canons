# Atoms & the periodic table

*c. 1800–1900*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Dalton revived the ancient idea of atoms as a precise chemical theory, with each element made of atoms of a characteristic weight. As more elements were found and their weights measured, chemists sought order among them, which Mendeleev found in the periodic table and used to predict elements still unknown.

## Scientists

-  [John Dalton](../creators/dalton.md) (1766 – 1844)
-  [Dmitri Mendeleev](../creators/mendeleev.md) (1834 – 1907)
