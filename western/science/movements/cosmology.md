# Cosmology & the expanding universe

*c. 1915–1965*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

General relativity made the shape and history of the universe a scientific question. Hubble showed that the spiral nebulae are galaxies far beyond our own and that they recede from us, and Lemaître proposed that the universe began in a hot, dense state, the idea later called the Big Bang.

## Scientists

-  [Albert Einstein](../creators/einstein.md) (1879 – 1955)
-  [Edwin Hubble](../creators/hubble.md) (1889 – 1953)
-  [Georges Lemaître](../creators/lemaitre.md) (1894 – 1966)
