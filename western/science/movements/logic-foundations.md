# Logic & the foundations of mathematics

*c. 1854–1936*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

Logic was made into algebra and then into a precise language for all of mathematics. Hilbert hoped to secure mathematics completely through formal proof, but Gödel showed that any such system must leave some truths unprovable, and Turing's analysis of computation followed directly from the same questions.

-  [George Boole](../creators/boole.md) (1815 – 1864)
-  [Georg Cantor](../creators/cantor.md) (1845 – 1918)
