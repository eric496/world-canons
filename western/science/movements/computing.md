# Computing & information

*c. 1822–1950*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

Babbage designed mechanical engines for calculation and Lovelace saw that they could manipulate any symbols, not only numbers. A century later Turing defined computation itself, wartime codebreaking and the first electronic computers made it real, and Shannon's theory of information gave the digital age its mathematics.
