# Medieval Latin scholarship

*c. 1150–1450*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

The new universities of Paris, Oxford and Bologna absorbed the translated works of Aristotle, Euclid, Ptolemy and their Arabic commentators. Scholars such as Roger Bacon and Nicole Oresme argued for mathematics and experience in natural knowledge, analysed motion and questioned whether the Earth stood still, while Fibonacci brought Hindu-Arabic arithmetic to European merchants.
