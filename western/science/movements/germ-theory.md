# Public health, germ theory & modern medicine

*c. 1840–1945*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Statistics and mapping revealed how disease spread, and the work of Pasteur and Koch proved that many diseases are caused by specific microbes. Antiseptic surgery, new vaccines and an understanding of the nervous system followed, and in the twentieth century the discovery of penicillin began the antibiotic era.
