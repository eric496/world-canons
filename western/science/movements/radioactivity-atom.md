# X-rays, radioactivity & the atom

*c. 1895–1932*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

Within a few years of 1895, X-rays, radioactivity and the electron were discovered, and the indivisible atom of the chemists turned out to have structure. Marie Curie isolated new radioactive elements, Rutherford found the tiny atomic nucleus, and Bohr's quantum model of the atom explained the spectra of light that elements emit.

## Scientists

-  [Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen](../creators/rontgen.md) (1845 – 1923)
-  [Marie Curie](../creators/marie-curie.md) (1867 – 1934)
-  [Ernest Rutherford](../creators/rutherford.md) (1871 – 1937)
