# Relativity & quantum mechanics

*c. 1900–1935*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

Two revolutions overturned Newtonian physics at its extremes. Planck's quantum and Einstein's relativity, special and general, changed the meaning of energy, space, time and gravity, and in the 1920s Heisenberg, Schrödinger and others built quantum mechanics, a theory of great predictive power whose interpretation is debated to this day.
