# Enlightenment chemistry & natural history

*c. 1735–1810*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

The eighteenth century classified and measured. Linnaeus named the living world, Franklin showed lightning to be electrical, Lavoisier founded modern chemistry on careful weighing, Laplace extended Newtonian mechanics to the whole solar system, and Jenner's vaccine opened the age of preventive medicine. Humboldt's travels joined it all into a vision of nature as an interconnected whole.
