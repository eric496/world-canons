# Mathematics in the age of rigour

*c. 1800–1900*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Mathematicians rebuilt their subject on firmer foundations and pushed it into new territory. Gauss transformed number theory and geometry, Riemann reimagined space itself, Cantor created the theory of infinite sets, and new algebraic structures appeared that would later prove essential to physics.
