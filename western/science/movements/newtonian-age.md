# The Newtonian age

*c. 1687–1760*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

Newton's Principia united earthly and heavenly motion under one law of gravitation, and with Leibniz the calculus became the language of physics. Over the following decades Euler and others extended the new mathematics, while practical problems such as finding longitude at sea drove precision instrument-making.
