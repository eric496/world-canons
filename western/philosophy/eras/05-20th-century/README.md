# 20th Century to Present (1900 – )

## Era overview: analytic vs continental split
Labels retrospective/rough — useful if not taken literally.
- Analytic (dominant Britain, US, Australasia, Scandinavia): from Frege's new logic + Russell/Moore revolt against British Idealism at Cambridge. Method: break problem into small pieces, define terms precisely, test arguments step by step; model disciplines logic, maths, natural sciences. Suspicion: grand puzzles are confusions from sloppy language — clarify and puzzle dissolves/becomes answerable. Products: short argument-dense articles; philosophy of language, mind, logic, science; later rigorous political philosophy (Rawls).
- Continental (dominant Germany/France, named from British viewpoint): from Kant, Hegel, Marx, Nietzsche, then Husserl's phenomenology. Method: describe lived experience from inside; read ideas historically — products of time/society/power needing interpretation (hermeneutics) or unmasking (genealogy, critique). Suspicion: detached timeless viewpoint doesn't exist; thinker is inside history, language, body. Products: long difficult books; phenomenology, existentialism, critical theory, structuralism/poststructuralism.
- Why hardened: geography/catastrophe — Vienna Circle dispersed after Schlick's murder (1936) and Nazi rise; Carnap and Feigl emigrated (joined in the US by Hempel, from Reichenbach's Berlin group), reshaping US philosophy of science analytically. Heidegger, Frankfurt exiles, post-war French developed continental line. Often literally different ancestors (Frege/Russell vs Hegel/Nietzsche/Husserl).
- Caveats: split is about starting points/style, not nationality/intelligence. Wittgenstein Austrian in Cambridge; Popper Viennese critic of Vienna Circle; Beauvoir read by both; Anscombe was Wittgenstein's translator AND founder of analytic virtue-ethics revival. Best figures are bridges.
- Century in one sentence: analytic asked "what can we say, clearly and truthfully?"; continental asked "what is it like to be the creature doing the saying, inside history and power?" — most interesting work where they collide.

## Analytic thread
Logic first: Frege's new logic → Russell and Moore's revolt against British Idealism → early Wittgenstein's picture theory gives the movement its ideal of clarity → the Vienna Circle turns clarity into a program (meaning as verification) → Ayer exports it to English readers → late Wittgenstein and Ryle dissolve the program from inside (language-games, category mistakes) → Quine removes its two dogmas → philosophy of science (Popper against the Circle, then Kuhn against Popper) and philosophy of language become the growth areas → Rawls revives substantive political philosophy, and Anscombe and Foot revive virtue ethics, inside the analytic idiom.

## Phenomenology / existentialism thread
Family tree: Husserl invents phenomenology → Heidegger (assistant/successor) transforms into philosophy of Being → Sartre & Beauvoir (studied both) turn into French existentialism → Arendt (studied with Husserl, Heidegger; doctorate under Jaspers) turns toward politics. Heidegger breaks with Husserl; Beauvoir corrects Sartre; Arendt answers Heidegger's death-focus with birth-focus.

## Critical theory, poststructuralism, ethics revival
Marx, Freud, and Weber, institutionalized: the Frankfurt Institute (founded 1923) joins philosophy to social research → exile under Nazism → Horkheimer and Adorno ask why enlightenment itself turns into domination (the culture industry) → Marcuse carries the critique to the New Left → Habermas rebuilds it on communication rather than production. The French line runs in parallel: structuralism's decentered subject → Foucault's genealogies of power/knowledge in prisons, clinics, and sexuality → Derrida's deconstruction of the binaries texts rest on. Meanwhile, in English, Anscombe's 1958 manifesto and Foot's naturalism revive virtue ethics — a continental-sized question (how should one live?) asked with analytic tools.

## Learning order within era (five passes, not chronological)
Pass 1 friendly doors: Russell Problems → Sartre Humanism + No Exit, Beauvoir Ethics of Ambiguity → Kuhn Structure → Popper "Conjectures and Refutations" ch.1 (then argue Popper vs Kuhn).
Pass 2 language: Ayer chs.1,6 (the English popularization of positivism) → early Wittgenstein Tractatus excerpts guided → late Wittgenstein Investigations §§1–133. Arc IS analytic story.
Pass 3 experience/existence: Husserl Cartesian Meditations + guide (Zahavi) → Heidegger Basic Writings then guided Being and Time excerpts → re-read Sartre/Beauvoir with machinery, add Second Sex (Intro + Vol II opening, 2010 translation).
Pass 4 politics/power (rival lenses contrasted): Rawls Restatement (fairness) → Arendt Human Condition I,V then Eichmann in context (action among plural equals) → Frankfurt "Culture Industry" (culture as domination) → Foucault Discipline opening + Sexuality Vol.1 (power inside institutions/selves). Comparing Rawls→Arendt→Foucault teaches more than any alone.
Pass 5 hard core + rebuild: Derrida "Différance" + "Letter to a Japanese Friend", then Grammatology excerpts → Anscombe "Modern Moral Philosophy" + Foot trolley/Natural Goodness opening — era hands over to contemporary ethics.

## Oversimplifications (general)
- "Analytic = cold logic/science; continental = poetic nonsense" caricatures both (analytic produced Rawls/Anscombe; continental produced Husserl's rigorous science of experience). Difference is problems/method, not seriousness.
- Plus per-figure cautions above.
- Flattened dates: Tractatus 1921 German / 1922 English; Popper Logik 1934 but English 1959; Dialectic of Enlightenment circulated 1944, published 1947; Second Sex 1949 French, complete English only 2010.
