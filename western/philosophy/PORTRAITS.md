# Portrait gallery

[← Philosophy](README.md) · [Western canon](../README.md) · [All people](../PEOPLE.md)

Public-domain portraits of the philosophers in this map: busts, coins, manuscript illuminations, paintings and engravings, all from Wikimedia Commons. Ancient and medieval likenesses are mostly imagined or idealised. Photographs are not used, so many modern philosophers have no portrait here.
