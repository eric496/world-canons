# French Classicism

*c. 1630–1700*

Era: [Baroque & the Scientific Revolution](../../periods/seventeenth-century.md)

In the France of Louis XIV, writers sought clarity, order and decorum modelled on ancient rules. Corneille and Racine wrote verse tragedies observing the unities of time, place and action, Molière created a comedy of character and manners, and La Fontaine and La Rochefoucauld refined the fable and the maxim. The period set standards of taste for much of Europe.
