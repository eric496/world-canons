# Latin American Boom

*c. 1940–1980*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

From the 1960s, Latin American novelists won a worldwide readership with ambitious, experimental fiction. Borges had prepared the ground with his labyrinthine stories; García Márquez, Cortázar, Fuentes and Vargas Llosa followed, and the magical realism of One Hundred Years of Solitude became internationally famous. Neruda's poetry gave the continent an equally powerful voice.
