# Elizabethan & Jacobean

*c. 1570–1642*

Era: [The Renaissance](../../periods/renaissance.md)

Under Elizabeth I and James I, English literature had an extraordinary flowering. Public playhouses in London staged the work of Marlowe, Shakespeare and Jonson, the sonnet and the lyric flourished, and John Donne led the metaphysical poets with their dense, witty conceits. The period ended when Parliament closed the theatres in 1642.

## Authors
