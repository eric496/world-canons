# Naturalism

*c. 1870–1910*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Naturalism pushed realism towards science, presenting characters shaped by heredity and environment. Zola, its leader, documented the working class in his Rougon-Macquart cycle; Ibsen brought social problems onto the stage; Hardy portrayed people crushed by circumstance. Its frankness about sex, poverty and disease caused repeated scandals.

## Authors

-  [Émile Zola](../creators/zola.md) (1840 – 1902)
