# Sturm und Drang & Weimar Classicism

*c. 1770–1832*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

A generation of young German writers rebelled against French rules with works of passion, genius and nature: the Sturm und Drang, or Storm and Stress. Goethe's Werther became a European sensation. Goethe and Schiller later settled in Weimar and sought a balanced classicism inspired by ancient Greece, creating the foundations of modern German literature.
