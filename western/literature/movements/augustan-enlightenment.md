# Augustan & Enlightenment

*c. 1700–1780*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

Eighteenth-century writers valued reason, wit and public usefulness. In England Swift and Pope practised savage and polished satire; in France Voltaire and Rousseau carried Enlightenment ideas to a wide readership through tales, letters and novels. Literature became part of a new public sphere of coffee houses, periodicals and salons.
