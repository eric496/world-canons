# Medieval epic & romance

*c. 700–1400*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

Medieval Europe produced heroic poetry in its new vernacular languages, from the Old English Beowulf to the Old French chansons de geste such as the Song of Roland. In the twelfth century the courts of France invented romance: tales of knights, quests and love, above all the Arthurian stories of Chrétien de Troyes. Courtly love, chivalry and the Grail became lasting themes of European imagination.
