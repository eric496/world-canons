# Renaissance humanism

*c. 1400–1600*

Era: [The Renaissance](../../periods/renaissance.md)

Humanist scholars recovered and printed the classics, and writers used them to fashion new forms in their own languages. Ariosto's chivalric epic, Rabelais's comic giants and Montaigne's essays show the period's mix of learning, play and self-examination. Printing spread books more widely than ever before, and national literatures began to take their modern shape.
