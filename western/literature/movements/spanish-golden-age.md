# Spanish Golden Age

*c. 1550–1680*

Era: [Baroque & the Scientific Revolution](../../periods/seventeenth-century.md)

At the height of Spanish power, Spain's writers produced the Siglo de Oro. Cervantes wrote Don Quixote, often called the first modern novel; Lope de Vega created a popular national theatre with hundreds of plays; Calderón brought it to philosophical depth. Picaresque novels, mystical poetry and the comedia shaped literature across Europe.
