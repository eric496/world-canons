# Postmodernism

*c. 1955–2000*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

Postmodern writers treated fiction as a game aware of its own artifice, mixing genres, parodying traditions and questioning the possibility of a single truth. Nabokov, Calvino and Borges are among its precursors and masters. Later writers such as Toni Morrison combined formal experiment with history and memory, especially the history of those left out of the older canon.

## Authors
