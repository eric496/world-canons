# Romanticism

*c. 1790–1850*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Romantic writers prized imagination, feeling, nature and the individual over reason and convention. In England Blake, Wordsworth, Coleridge, Byron, Shelley and Keats remade poetry; in France Hugo led the revolt against classicism; in Russia Pushkin created a national literature. The Gothic novel, the historical novel and the cult of the outcast hero all belong to this movement.
