# Italian Trecento

*c. 1290–1400*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

Fourteenth-century Tuscany produced the three crowns of Italian literature. Dante's Divine Comedy proved that a vernacular could carry the highest subjects; Petrarch's sonnets created a language of love that the whole of Europe imitated; Boccaccio's Decameron set the pattern for the prose tale. Their influence, through Chaucer and the humanists, carried far beyond Italy.
