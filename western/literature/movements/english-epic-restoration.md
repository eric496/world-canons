# Milton & the Restoration

*c. 1640–1700*

Era: [Baroque & the Scientific Revolution](../../periods/seventeenth-century.md)

The English Civil War and the restoration of the monarchy in 1660 framed a turbulent literary era. Milton, the blind republican, wrote Paradise Lost, the great English epic; after 1660 reopened theatres staged witty Restoration comedy and Dryden made the heroic couplet the dominant verse form. It bridges the Renaissance and the Augustan age.
