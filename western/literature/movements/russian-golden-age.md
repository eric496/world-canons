# Russian Golden Age

*c. 1820–1905*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

In less than a century Russia produced one of the world's great literatures. Pushkin and Gogol laid its foundations; Tolstoy and Dostoevsky wrote vast novels of history, faith and conscience; Chekhov transformed the short story and the play. Under censorship and autocracy, literature became the main forum for Russia's moral and political debates.
