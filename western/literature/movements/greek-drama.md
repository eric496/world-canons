# Greek tragedy & comedy

*c. 500–380 BC*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

Drama was born in Athens at the festivals of Dionysus, where citizen choruses and a few masked actors performed new plays in competition. Aeschylus, Sophocles and Euripides turned myth into tragedy that questioned justice, fate and the gods, while Aristophanes wrote riotous political comedy. Aristotle's Poetics, written a century later, made these plays the template for Western theories of drama.
