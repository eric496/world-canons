# Rise of the novel

*c. 1700–1800*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

Long prose fiction about ordinary people became the dominant new form of the century, especially in England. Defoe's realistic adventures, Richardson's novels in letters, Fielding's comic epics and Sterne's playful Tristram Shandy found a growing middle-class and female readership. Circulating libraries and cheaper printing made the novel the most popular literary genre in Europe.
