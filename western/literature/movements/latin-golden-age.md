# Latin Golden Age

*c. 70 BC – AD 18*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

Under the late Republic and the reign of Augustus, Roman poets reshaped Greek models into a confident Latin literature. Virgil wrote the national epic, the Aeneid; Horace perfected the ode and the verse letter; Ovid retold the whole of mythology in the Metamorphoses. For more than fifteen centuries these were the school texts of Europe, the core of what an educated person read.

## Authors
