# Realism

*c. 1830–1890*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Realist novelists set out to show society as it actually was, especially the middle classes, money, marriage and work. Balzac mapped France in his Human Comedy, Flaubert perfected the impersonal style, and in Britain Dickens, the Brontës and George Eliot built large social and moral panoramas. The realist novel became the century's central art form.
