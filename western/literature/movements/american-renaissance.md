# American Renaissance

*c. 1835–1865*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

In the decades before the Civil War, American writers created a literature independent of Britain. Poe invented the detective story and refined the tale of terror, Hawthorne explored Puritan guilt, Melville wrote Moby-Dick and Whitman Leaves of Grass. Emily Dickinson, writing in private, belongs to the same remarkable generation.
