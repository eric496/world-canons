# Symbolism & Aestheticism

*c. 1857–1910*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Starting with Baudelaire, French poets sought suggestion, music and correspondence rather than description, and Rimbaud, Verlaine and Mallarmé pushed language to new limits. In Britain the Aesthetic movement, with Oscar Wilde as its showman, proclaimed art for art's sake. Their ideas fed directly into Modernism and twentieth-century poetry.
