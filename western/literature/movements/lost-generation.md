# Lost Generation & American modernism

*c. 1920–1950*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

The term Lost Generation, used by Gertrude Stein and popularised by Hemingway, describes American writers who came of age in the First World War, many of whom lived in Paris in the 1920s. Fitzgerald chronicled the Jazz Age, Hemingway developed a terse new prose and Faulkner reinvented the Southern novel. Their styles shaped American fiction for decades.

## Authors

-  [F. Scott Fitzgerald](../creators/fitzgerald.md) (1896 – 1940)
-  [William Faulkner](../creators/faulkner.md) (1897 – 1962)
-  [Ernest Hemingway](../creators/hemingway.md) (1899 – 1961)
