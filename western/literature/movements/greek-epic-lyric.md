# Greek epic & lyric

*c. 750–450 BC*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

Greek literature begins with the oral epics attributed to Homer, the Iliad and the Odyssey, composed in dactylic hexameter and performed by singers long before they were written down. Alongside them grew a lyric tradition of shorter sung poems, personal, erotic or choral, whose greatest voice was Sappho of Lesbos. Together they supplied the stories, metres and models that all later European poetry drew on.
