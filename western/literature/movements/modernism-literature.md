# Modernism

*c. 1900–1945*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

Modernist writers broke with traditional narrative and verse to capture consciousness, memory and the fractured experience of modern life. Proust, Joyce, Woolf, Kafka, Mann, Rilke and T. S. Eliot experimented with stream of consciousness, myth, fragmentation and new forms. The First World War sharpened their sense that an old world had ended.
