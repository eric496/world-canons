# Existentialism & the Absurd

*c. 1940–1965*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

After the Second World War, writers confronted a world without given meaning. Camus explored the absurd and revolt in novels and essays, Sartre made existentialism a literary movement, and the Theatre of the Absurd of Beckett and Ionesco stripped drama down to waiting, repetition and dark comedy. Orwell's political fiction belongs to the same era of totalitarian threat.
