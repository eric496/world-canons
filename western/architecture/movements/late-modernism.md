# Late & Expressive Modernism

*c. 1945–1975*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

After the war, modernism became the official style of corporations and governments, while many architects pushed it toward sculptural concrete shells, monumental geometry and regional character. Niemeyer's Brasília, Saarinen's TWA terminal, Kahn's monumental buildings and Utzon's Sydney Opera House belong here.
