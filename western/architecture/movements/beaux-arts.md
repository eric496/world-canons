# Beaux-Arts & Historicism

*c. 1850–1920*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Taught at the École des Beaux-Arts in Paris, this academic style combined rigorous symmetrical planning with rich classical and Baroque decoration. Opera houses, museums, libraries and railway terminals across Europe and the Americas were built in its grand manner.
