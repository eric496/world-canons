# Palladianism & Neoclassicism

*c. 1615–1850*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

Palladio's villas and treatise inspired Inigo Jones and, a century later, a British and American Palladian revival. From the 1750s, archaeology at Herculaneum, Pompeii and Athens encouraged a sterner, purer classicism. Neoclassicism became the language of the Enlightenment and of the new republics, museums and capitals.
