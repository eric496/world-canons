# Early Christian & Byzantine

*c. 300–1453*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

After Constantine, Christians adapted the Roman basilica for congregational worship and built centrally planned shrines and baptisteries. In the Eastern Empire, builders set domes over square bays on pendentives and covered interiors in gold mosaic, culminating in Justinian's Hagia Sophia. The Byzantine domed church spread through Italy, the Balkans and Russia.
