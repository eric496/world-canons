# Brutalism

*c. 1950–1980*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

Taking its name from béton brut, the raw concrete of Le Corbusier's Unité d'Habitation, Brutalism exposed materials and structure with blunt honesty. It was widely used for housing, universities and civic buildings in the postwar welfare states, and remains much debated, demolished and, lately, admired.
