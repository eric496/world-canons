# Iron, Glass & Engineering

*c. 1830–1900*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Industrial iron, steel and plate glass gave rise to a new architecture of greenhouses, railway sheds, exhibition halls, bridges and towers, often designed by engineers rather than architects. The Crystal Palace and the Eiffel Tower showed that prefabricated metal structures could be spectacular in their own right.

## Architects

-  [Joseph Paxton](../creators/paxton.md) (1803 – 1865)
-  [Gustave Eiffel](../creators/eiffel.md) (1832 – 1923)
