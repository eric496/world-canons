# Carolingian & Romanesque

*c. 780–1150*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

From Charlemagne's chapel at Aachen onward, northern Europe revived Roman ideas of masonry building. Romanesque churches, raised along pilgrimage roads and by great monasteries, have thick walls, round arches, massive piers and stone vaults, with sculpted portals and capitals. Durham Cathedral's ribbed vaults point toward the Gothic.
