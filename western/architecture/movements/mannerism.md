# Mannerism

*c. 1520–1600*

Era: [The Renaissance](../../periods/renaissance.md)

Architects who had mastered the classical rules began to bend them: columns sunk into walls, brackets that support nothing, keystones that slip. Michelangelo and Giulio Romano treated the orders with wit and tension, while Vignola and the Counter-Reformation produced the model of the Jesuit church.
