# Art Deco

*c. 1920–1940*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

Named after the 1925 Paris decorative arts exhibition, Art Deco brought streamlined, geometric ornament, stepped profiles and luxurious materials to cinemas, liners and above all New York skyscrapers. It celebrated speed, machinery and modern glamour.
