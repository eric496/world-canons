# Postmodernism

*c. 1965–1995*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

Reacting against the austerity of modernism, postmodern architects brought back ornament, colour, historical quotation and irony. Robert Venturi's 'less is a bore' countered Mies's 'less is more', and buildings began to speak in signs and symbols again.
