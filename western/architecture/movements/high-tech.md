# High-tech

*c. 1970–present*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

High-tech architects celebrate engineering, exposing structure, ducts and services and using lightweight steel, glass and prefabricated parts. Born in Britain and France with the Centre Pompidou, it shaped many corporate towers and airports and evolved toward environmental performance.
