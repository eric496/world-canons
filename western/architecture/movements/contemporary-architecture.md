# Contemporary

*c. 1990–present*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

Today's architecture is plural: expressive structure, material experiment, adaptive reuse of industrial buildings and growing concern with energy and climate. Signature cultural buildings compete with a quieter return to craft, context and sustainability.
