# Deconstructivism & Digital Form

*c. 1980–present*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

Named after a 1988 exhibition at the Museum of Modern Art, deconstructivist buildings fragment, tilt and collide forms, rejecting the stable box. Computer design and fabrication then made possible the flowing, curved forms of Gehry and Hadid.

## Architects

-  [Frank Gehry](../creators/gehry.md) (1929 – 2025)
-  [Rem Koolhaas](../creators/koolhaas.md) (1944 – )
-  [Daniel Libeskind](../creators/libeskind.md) (1946 – )
-  [Zaha Hadid](../creators/hadid.md) (1950 – 2016)
