# Gothic

*c. 1140–1520*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

Beginning at Saint-Denis near Paris, the pointed arch, rib vault and flying buttress let builders raise taller churches and dissolve their walls into stained glass. The style spread from the Île-de-France across Europe, producing High Gothic cathedrals, the Rayonnant glass cage of the Sainte-Chapelle and the late flamboyant, perpendicular and hall-church variants.
