# Ancient Roman

*c. 300 BC – AD 400*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

Rome borrowed the Greek orders but built with the arch, the vault and the dome, using concrete to enclose vast interior spaces. Basilicas, baths, amphitheatres, aqueducts and triumphal arches served an empire of cities. Vitruvius wrote the only architectural treatise to survive from antiquity, which shaped Western theory for over a thousand years.
