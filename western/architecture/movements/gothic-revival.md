# Gothic Revival

*c. 1740–1900*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

First a picturesque taste for medieval ruins and castles, the Gothic Revival became a moral and national cause in the nineteenth century. Pugin argued that pointed architecture was the only true Christian style; Viollet-le-Duc studied and restored medieval buildings as rational structure. Parliaments, churches, universities and railway stations went Gothic.
