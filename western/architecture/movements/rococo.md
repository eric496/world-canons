# Rococo

*c. 1720–1770*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

A lighter, more decorative late phase of the Baroque that began in French interiors. Rococo rooms dissolve structure into white and gold stucco, asymmetrical shell-like ornament, mirrors and painted skies. In southern Germany, Austria and Russia it produced pilgrimage churches and palaces of extraordinary brilliance.
