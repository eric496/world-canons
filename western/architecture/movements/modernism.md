# Modernism & the International Style

*c. 1905–1960*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

Modernists rejected historical ornament in favour of form that followed function, new materials and the free plan made possible by the frame. Wright's organic architecture, De Stijl, the Bauhaus and Le Corbusier's five points converged by the late 1920s on a white, flat-roofed, glass-walled International Style that went worldwide after 1945.
