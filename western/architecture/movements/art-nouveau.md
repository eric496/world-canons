# Art Nouveau & Modernisme

*c. 1890–1914*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

A deliberate break with historical styles, Art Nouveau took its forms from plants, whiplash curves and new materials such as exposed iron. It flourished in Brussels, Paris, Glasgow, Vienna and Barcelona, where Gaudí's Catalan Modernisme went furthest. The Vienna Secession pointed toward a sparer, geometric modernity.
