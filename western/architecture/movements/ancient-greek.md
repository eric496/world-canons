# Ancient Greek

*c. 650–30 BC*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

Greek builders turned the timber temple into stone and refined it into the Doric, Ionic and Corinthian orders: column, capital and entablature in fixed proportions. Temples were conceived as sculpture set in a landscape, with subtle curves and tapers to correct the eye. Theatres, stoas and sanctuaries completed the repertoire that every later classicism would return to.
