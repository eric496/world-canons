# Baroque

*c. 1600–1750*

Era: [Baroque & the Scientific Revolution](../../periods/seventeenth-century.md)

Born in papal Rome, Baroque architecture uses curving walls, ovals, dramatic light and the fusion of architecture, sculpture and painting to move the viewer. It served the Catholic Church and absolute monarchs alike, from Bernini's colonnade at St Peter's to Versailles, Wren's London and the palaces and abbeys of Central Europe.
