# Chicago School

*c. 1880–1910*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

After the fire of 1871, Chicago builders combined steel frames, elevators and fireproofing to produce the first skyscrapers. Louis Sullivan gave the tall office building an artistic form, organised as base, shaft and crown, and the large 'Chicago window' became a hallmark.
