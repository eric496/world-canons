# 20th-century theology & ecumenism

*c. 1914–1970*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

Two world wars and the Holocaust prompted searching theology: Barth's dialectical theology, Bonhoeffer's resistance writings, Tillich's existential theology and Buber's philosophy of dialogue. The ecumenical movement brought churches together in the World Council of Churches (1948), and the Second Vatican Council (1962–65) reshaped Catholicism. Religion also drove movements for social justice, most visibly the American civil rights movement.
