# Catholic Reformation

*c. 1530–1650*

Era: [The Renaissance](../../periods/renaissance.md)

The Roman Catholic Church responded to Protestantism and to its own reform currents with the Council of Trent, new religious orders such as the Jesuits, and a flowering of mysticism in Spain. Historians debate whether to call it the Counter-Reformation, stressing reaction, or the Catholic Reformation, stressing renewal. It shaped Catholic worship, education and missions for four centuries.
