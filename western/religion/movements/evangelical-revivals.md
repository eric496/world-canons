# Evangelical revivals

*c. 1730–1850*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

Field preaching, hymn singing and a stress on personal conversion swept Britain and North America in the Great Awakening and the Methodist movement led by John and Charles Wesley and George Whitefield. Jonathan Edwards gave the revivals a theologian. In the same period Hasidism arose among eastern European Jews, and Jewish thinkers such as Moses Mendelssohn engaged the Enlightenment.
