# Mendicant orders

*c. 1200–1500*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

The Franciscans and Dominicans, founded in the early 13th century, combined vows of poverty with preaching in the growing towns. Francis of Assisi's ideal of evangelical poverty and Dominic's order of preachers produced many of the leading theologians of the universities, including Aquinas and Bonaventure. Debates over poverty and over the friars' privileges ran through the late Middle Ages.
