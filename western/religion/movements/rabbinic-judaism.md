# Rabbinic Judaism

*c. 70–600*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

After the destruction of the Temple, the rabbis reorganised Jewish life around Torah study, prayer and law. Their oral teaching was codified in the Mishnah around 200 and expanded in the Jerusalem and Babylonian Talmuds, together with collections of scriptural interpretation called midrash. This rabbinic tradition became normative Judaism for the following millennium and a half.
