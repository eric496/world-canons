# 19th-century theology

*c. 1799–1914*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Faced with Enlightenment criticism, historical scholarship of the Bible and modern science, theologians reworked religion around feeling, history and ethics. Schleiermacher founded liberal Protestant theology, Kierkegaard insisted on individual faith, and Newman moved from Anglicanism to Rome. Judaism divided into Reform, Orthodox and other movements, and new missionary and social movements multiplied.
