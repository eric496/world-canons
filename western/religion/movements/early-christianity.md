# Early Christianity & the New Testament

*c. AD 30–325*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

Followers of Jesus of Nazareth spread their movement from Judea across the Roman Empire, at first as a Jewish sect and increasingly among gentiles. Paul's letters and the four Gospels, written in Greek within roughly a century of Jesus's death, were gathered with other texts into the New Testament. Christians faced periodic persecution until Constantine's toleration in 313.
