# Monasticism

*c. 270–1100*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

Christian ascetics withdrew first into the Egyptian and Syrian deserts and then into organised communities living under a rule of prayer, work and obedience. The Rule of St Benedict became the standard in the Latin West, and Irish and Anglo-Saxon monasteries became centres of learning and book production. Monasteries preserved texts, ran estates and sent out missionaries across Europe.
