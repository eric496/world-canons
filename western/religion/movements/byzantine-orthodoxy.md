# Byzantine Orthodoxy

*c. 330–1453*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

The Greek-speaking Christianity of the Eastern Roman Empire, centred on Constantinople, developed its own liturgy, iconography, mystical theology and church law. The iconoclast controversies of the 8th and 9th centuries ended with the restoration of icons, and missions carried Orthodoxy to the Slavs. Tensions with Rome culminated in the schism traditionally dated to 1054.
