# Second Temple Judaism

*c. 515 BC – AD 70*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

Between the rebuilding of the Jerusalem Temple and its destruction by Rome, Judaism was diverse: Pharisees, Sadducees, Essenes and others debated law, purity and the end of days. The period produced the Greek Septuagint, apocalyptic writings, the Dead Sea Scrolls and the Hellenistic Jewish philosophy of Philo. Both Rabbinic Judaism and Christianity grew out of it.
