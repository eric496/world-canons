# Medieval scholasticism & mysticism

*c. 1050–1450*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

In cathedral schools and new universities, scholastics such as Anselm, Peter Lombard and Aquinas used logic and recovered Aristotle to systematise Christian doctrine. Alongside them a rich mystical literature, from Bernard and Hildegard to Eckhart, Julian of Norwich and the Devotio Moderna, explored the soul's direct experience of God, often in the vernacular.
