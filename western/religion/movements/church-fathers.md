# Church Fathers & the Councils

*c. 100–750*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

Theologians writing in Greek, Latin and Syriac defined Christian doctrine, interpreted scripture and argued against rivals they considered heretical. The ecumenical councils from Nicaea (325) to Chalcedon (451) and beyond fixed the creeds on the Trinity and the nature of Christ. Origen, Athanasius, the Cappadocians, Jerome and Augustine shaped all later theology, East and West.
