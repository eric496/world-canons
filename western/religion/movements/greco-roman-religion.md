# Greco-Roman religion

*c. 800 BC – AD 400*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

The polytheistic religion of the Greek and Roman worlds centred on sacrifice, festivals, oracles and civic cults rather than a single sacred book. Poets such as Homer and Hesiod gave the gods their stories, while mystery cults such as those of Eleusis, Isis and Mithras offered personal initiation. Its temples, myths and ideas about the divine passed into later Western culture even after Christianity displaced it.
