# Ancient Israel & the Hebrew Bible

*c. 1000–400 BC*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

The kingdoms of Israel and Judah and the exile in Babylon form the setting in which the books of the Hebrew Bible were composed, edited and collected. Tradition ascribes the Torah to Moses and the Psalms to David; most modern scholars see the texts as the work of many hands over centuries, taking much of their final shape during and after the Babylonian exile. The result became the scripture of Judaism and the Old Testament of Christianity.
