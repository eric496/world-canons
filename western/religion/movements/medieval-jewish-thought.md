# Medieval Jewish thought

*c. 900–1500*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

Jewish scholars in the Islamic world, Spain, Provence and northern France produced biblical and talmudic commentary, legal codes, philosophy and poetry. Rashi's commentaries, Maimonides's code and philosophy, Judah Halevi's Kuzari and the Kabbalah of the Zohar became classics. This creativity unfolded amid periods of tolerance and of persecution, culminating in the expulsion from Spain in 1492.
