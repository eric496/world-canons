# Protestant Reformation

*c. 1517–1600*

Era: [The Renaissance](../../periods/renaissance.md)

Building on humanist scholarship and earlier reform movements, Luther, Zwingli, Calvin and others broke with the papacy and taught justification by faith and the authority of scripture. Printing spread vernacular Bibles, catechisms and hymns. The result was a lasting division of Western Christianity into Lutheran, Reformed, Anglican and Anabaptist churches alongside Roman Catholicism.
