# Puritanism & Pietism

*c. 1560–1750*

Era: [Baroque & the Scientific Revolution](../../periods/seventeenth-century.md)

In England and New England, Puritans sought a further reformation of church and life according to scripture, producing a rich literature of sermons, diaries and allegory. In Lutheran Germany, Pietists such as Spener and Francke emphasised conversion, small-group Bible study and practical charity over doctrinal controversy. Both movements prepared the way for the evangelical revivals.
