## Notes on this section

- **Scope.** The written tradition of Western art music, from plainchant to about 1980, plus jazz as the twentieth century's other great Western musical art. Popular song, rock and film music are outside its scope, as is music after about 1980.
- **Dates.** A work's year is the year of composition; its sort date and the *Premiere / first published* field give the first performance or publication where they differ. Russian dates are in the Western (Gregorian) calendar.
- **Pictures.** Portraits are paintings, drawings, engravings or prints in the public domain, never photographs, so many twentieth-century composers have none. Work pictures show autograph manuscripts, first editions, title pages, playbills, set designs or the places of first performance. Music still under copyright is described but not reproduced.
- **Scores.** *Free scores on IMSLP* links open a search on the International Music Score Library Project, which hosts public-domain editions. What counts as public domain differs between countries; check the notice on each IMSLP page.
- **Listening.** No recording links are given, as availability changes; most of these works are easy to find on public radio archives and streaming services.
