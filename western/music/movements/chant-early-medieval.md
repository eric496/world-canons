# Chant & early medieval

*c. 600–1150*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

The single-line sacred song of the Latin church, sung in Latin to free, unmetred rhythms and organised by the eight church modes. Carolingian rulers spread a standard repertory, later credited to Pope Gregory I, and monks invented staff notation so it could be written down rather than memorised. Hymns, sequences and liturgical drama, like the visionary songs of Hildegard of Bingen, grew around this core.
