# Nationalist schools

*c. 1860–1920*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Composers from Russia, Bohemia, Scandinavia and elsewhere set out to create music of their own nations, drawing on folk song, dance rhythms, national history and landscape. The Russian 'Mighty Handful', with Mussorgsky at its most original, Smetana and Dvořák in Bohemia, Grieg in Norway and Sibelius in Finland gave this movement its classics.
