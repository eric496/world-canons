# Ars nova

*c. 1310–1380*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

A new notation, set out in treatises of around 1320, allowed duple as well as triple time and much finer rhythmic values. French composers used it for intricate isorhythmic motets and for courtly songs in the fixed forms of ballade, rondeau and virelai. Guillaume de Machaut, poet and composer, is its towering figure, and Italy developed a parallel trecento style.
