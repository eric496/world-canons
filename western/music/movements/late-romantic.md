# Late Romantic & post-Wagnerian

*c. 1880–1915*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Under Wagner's influence harmony became ever richer and more chromatic, and orchestras grew enormous. Mahler's vast symphonies, Bruckner's cathedral-like structures, Strauss's tone poems and operas and Puccini's verismo dramas belong to this last flowering of Romanticism, which stretched tonality close to breaking point.

## Composers

-  [Anton Bruckner](../creators/bruckner.md) (1824 – 1896)
-  [Giacomo Puccini](../creators/puccini.md) (1858 – 1924)
