# Jazz

*c. 1910–1970*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

Jazz arose among African American musicians in New Orleans in the early twentieth century, out of blues, ragtime, brass-band music and spirituals. Built on swing rhythm, blue notes and improvisation, it spread through recordings and radio to become a world music. Louis Armstrong made the improvising soloist central, Duke Ellington made the jazz orchestra a composer's instrument, and Gershwin carried its idioms into the concert hall.
