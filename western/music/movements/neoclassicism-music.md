# Neoclassicism & mid-century tonal modernism

*c. 1920–1960*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

After the First World War many composers rejected Romantic excess and returned to the clarity, forms and dry textures of the eighteenth century, filtered through modern harmony. Stravinsky led the way; Prokofiev, Copland, Shostakovich and Britten in different ways forged accessible but distinctly twentieth-century tonal styles, some of them under heavy political pressure.
