# Early Romantic

*c. 1815–1850*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Romantic composers prized personal expression, the sublime, nature and the supernatural, and links with poetry and literature. The art song, the short lyric piano piece and the programme symphony flourished, and the virtuoso performer became a celebrity. Schubert, Berlioz, Mendelssohn, Chopin, Schumann and the young Liszt worked in this generation, alongside the bel canto opera of Rossini and his successors.
