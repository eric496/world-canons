# Galant & Classical

*c. 1740–1820*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

Composers turned towards clear, singable melody over light accompaniment, first in the galant style and then in the Classical style of Vienna. Sonata form, the symphony, the string quartet and the piano concerto became the central genres, built on balanced phrases and dramatic contrasts of key. Haydn, Mozart and early Beethoven are its masters, and Gluck reformed opera towards dramatic truth.
