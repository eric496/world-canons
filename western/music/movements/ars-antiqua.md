# Ars antiqua (Notre-Dame school)

*c. 1160–1300*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

At the new cathedral of Notre-Dame in Paris, singers began to add one, two or three voices above a slowed-down chant, measuring them in repeating rhythmic patterns. Léonin and Pérotin are the first named composers of this polyphony, collected in the Magnus liber organi. The thirteenth century added the motet, in which each voice could sing a different text.

## Composers

-  [Léonin](../creators/leonin.md) (fl. c. 1150 – c. 1201)
-  [Pérotin](../creators/perotin.md) (fl. c. 1200 – c. 1230)
