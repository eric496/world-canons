# High Romantic & opera

*c. 1850–1890*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Opera dominated the middle of the century. Verdi brought Italian opera to a new dramatic intensity, while Wagner remade opera as music drama, with continuous music woven from leading motifs. Instrumental music split, in a famous controversy, between Liszt and Wagner's progressive New German School and the classical forms upheld by Brahms.

## Composers

-  [Franz Liszt](../creators/liszt.md) (1811 – 1886)
-  [Richard Wagner](../creators/wagner.md) (1813 – 1883)
-  [Giuseppe Verdi](../creators/verdi.md) (1813 – 1901)
