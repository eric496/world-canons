# High Baroque

*c. 1680–1750*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

Major-minor tonality became the governing system, giving music strong harmonic direction. Corelli and Vivaldi fixed the forms of the concerto and sonata, opera seria spread from Italy across Europe, and French music under Lully and Rameau kept its own dance-based style. Bach and Handel summed up the age, in learned counterpoint and in English oratorio.
