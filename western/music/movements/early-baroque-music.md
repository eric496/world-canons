# Early Baroque & opera

*c. 1600–1680*

Era: [Baroque & the Scientific Revolution](../../periods/seventeenth-century.md)

Around 1600 Florentine humanists, hoping to revive Greek drama, created recitative: a solo voice declaiming over a bass line. This monody gave birth to opera, and Monteverdi's Orfeo of 1607 showed its dramatic power. The basso continuo, the concerted church style, the cantata, the oratorio and the first independent instrumental sonatas all date from this period.
