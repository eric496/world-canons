# Franco-Flemish Renaissance

*c. 1420–1530*

Era: [The Renaissance](../../periods/renaissance.md)

Composers from northern France and the Low Countries dominated the courts and chapels of Europe for a century. Absorbing a sweet, consonant English sound, they built the cyclic mass on a borrowed tune and developed imitation, where each voice takes up the same motif in turn. Dufay, Ockeghem and Josquin des Prez are the central names, and music printing spread their work from 1501.
