# Impressionism & French modernism

*c. 1890–1930*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

French composers, led by Debussy, turned away from German rhetoric towards colour, atmosphere and suggestion. They used whole-tone and pentatonic scales, old modes, unresolved chords and delicate orchestral textures, often inspired by poetry, painting and the music of Java heard in Paris in 1889. Ravel and the ironic simplicity of Satie extended the French line.

## Composers

-  [Claude Debussy](../creators/debussy.md) (1862 – 1918)
-  [Erik Satie](../creators/satie.md) (1866 – 1925)
-  [Maurice Ravel](../creators/ravel.md) (1875 – 1937)
