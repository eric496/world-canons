# Late Renaissance & Venetian

*c. 1530–1600*

Era: [The Renaissance](../../periods/renaissance.md)

The mature polyphonic style reached its classic poise in the Roman church music of Palestrina and in the works of Lassus, Victoria, Tallis and Byrd. Reformation and Counter-Reformation both pressed for words that could be understood. The Italian madrigal grew ever more expressive, and at St Mark's in Venice the Gabrielis exploited divided choirs and brass in grand antiphonal sound.
