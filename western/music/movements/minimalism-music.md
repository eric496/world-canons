# Minimalism & new simplicity

*c. 1964–1990*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

In the 1960s American composers including Terry Riley, Steve Reich and Philip Glass reacted against the complexity of serialism with music built from steady pulse, repeated patterns and slow, audible processes of change, influenced by African drumming, Indian music and rock. In Estonia Arvo Pärt developed his spare, bell-like tintinnabuli style. These styles brought new audiences to contemporary music.
