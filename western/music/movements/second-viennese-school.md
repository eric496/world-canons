# Second Viennese School & modernism

*c. 1908–1950*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

Arnold Schoenberg and his pupils Alban Berg and Anton Webern left tonality behind, writing 'atonal' music without a key centre from 1908. In the early 1920s Schoenberg devised the twelve-tone method, which orders all twelve notes of the chromatic scale into a series. Alongside them, Stravinsky's Rite of Spring and Bartók's folk-based modernism shook the musical world with new rhythmic violence.
