# Post-war avant-garde

*c. 1945–1975*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

After 1945 young composers sought a fresh start. At Darmstadt, Boulez and Stockhausen extended serial ordering to every parameter of sound; electronic studios opened in Paris and Cologne; Messiaen drew on birdsong and Indian rhythm. John Cage introduced chance and silence, and Ligeti built shimmering clouds of sound. Their experiments widened the very idea of music.
