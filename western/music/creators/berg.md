## Biography

Alban Berg was born in Vienna in 1885 into a prosperous family. Largely self-taught, he began studying with Schoenberg in 1904 and remained his devoted friend. He served in the Austrian army during the First World War, an experience that shaped Wozzeck, the opera based on Büchner's fragmentary play, which premiered in Berlin in 1925 and made him famous. He lived quietly in Vienna, adopting the twelve-tone method in the Lyric Suite for string quartet (1926), which modern research has shown contains a secret programme about his love for Hanna Fuchs-Robettin. Under the Nazis his music was branded degenerate. He died in Vienna on Christmas Eve 1935 from an infected insect sting, leaving his second opera, Lulu, incomplete.

Berg's music is the most lyrical and approachable of the Second Viennese School. He used strict forms in unexpected ways (each scene of Wozzeck is built on a traditional form such as a passacaglia or suite) and often worked hidden numbers and ciphers into his scores. His twelve-tone rows often contain tonal triads, allowing reminiscences of tonality, as in the quotation of a Bach chorale in the Violin Concerto. Wozzeck is one of the most performed operas of the century.

### Wozzeck (1914–22)

- **Date:** 1914–22
- **Type:** Opera, three acts, Op. 7
- **Premiere / first published:** Berlin State Opera, 14 December 1925, conducted by Erich Kleiber
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Wozzeck%20Alban%20Berg)
- **Links:** [Free scores on IMSLP (search)](https://imslp.org/index.php?search=Wozzeck+Berg)

Berg saw Georg Büchner's fragmentary play Woyzeck in Vienna in 1914 and decided to set it. The soldier Wozzeck, abused by his Captain and used as a guinea pig by the Doctor, discovers that Marie, the mother of his child, is having an affair with a Drum Major. He stabs her by a pond and drowns trying to wash away the blood, while their son plays, unaware. Berg's experiences in the army during the First World War informed the work. Each of the fifteen scenes is based on a traditional musical form, such as a suite, a passacaglia or a sonata, though Berg did not want the audience to notice this. The music is largely atonal, with an orchestral interlude in D minor before the final scene. The premiere required 137 rehearsals. Wozzeck is one of the most important operas of the twentieth century.

### Violin Concerto, 'To the memory of an angel' (1935)

- **Date:** 1935
- **Type:** Violin concerto
- **Premiere / first published:** Palau de la Música Catalana, Barcelona, 19 April 1936, with Louis Krasner as soloist
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Violin%20Concerto%2C%20%27To%20the%20memory%20of%20an%20angel%27%20Alban%20Berg)
- **Links:** [Free scores on IMSLP (search)](https://imslp.org/index.php?search=Violin+Concerto+Berg)

The American violinist Louis Krasner commissioned a concerto from Berg, but he did not begin work until April 1935, when Manon Gropius, the eighteen-year-old daughter of Alma Mahler and the architect Walter Gropius, died of polio. Berg dedicated it to her memory. Its twelve-tone row is built from a chain of triads and ends with four rising whole-tone notes that coincide with the start of the Bach chorale Es ist genug (It is enough), which Berg quotes in its original harmonisation in the final movement. The concerto also includes a Carinthian folk song. Berg died before its premiere, so it became a requiem for him too.
