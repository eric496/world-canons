# Claude Debussy

**1862 – 1918** · French  
Born: Saint-Germain-en-Laye · Died: Paris  
Eras & schools: [Impressionism & French modernism](../movements/impressionism-music.md)  

> Debussy freed harmony from traditional rules and created a new music of colour and atmosphere, from Prélude à l'après-midi d'un faune to La mer.

**Links:** [Scores on IMSLP](https://imslp.org/wiki/Category:Debussy,_Claude)

## Biography

Claude Debussy was born in 1862 in Saint-Germain-en-Laye near Paris. He entered the Paris Conservatoire at ten, was a rebellious student of harmony, and in 1880–82 spent summers as house pianist to Tchaikovsky's patron Nadezhda von Meck. He won the Prix de Rome in 1884. At the 1889 Paris Exposition he heard a Javanese gamelan, which deeply influenced him. He moved in Symbolist literary circles and set poems by Baudelaire, Verlaine and Mallarmé, whose poem inspired Prélude à l'après-midi d'un faune (1894). His only completed opera, Pelléas et Mélisande, premiered in 1902. His private life caused scandal when he left his first wife for Emma Bardac. He died of cancer in Paris in 1918 during the German bombardment.

Debussy disliked the label Impressionism but sought in music something like the suggestion and nuance of the Symbolist poets and Impressionist painters. He used whole-tone and pentatonic scales, medieval modes, parallel chords and unresolved dissonance for their colour rather than their function, and preferred fluid forms to developmental argument. His Préludes and Images for piano, La mer and Jeux changed the course of twentieth-century music, influencing composers from Ravel and Stravinsky to Messiaen, Boulez and jazz musicians.

### Prélude à l'après-midi d'un faune (1892–94)

- **Date:** 1892–94
- **Type:** Orchestral prelude
- **Premiere / first published:** Société Nationale de Musique, Salle d'Harcourt, Paris, 22 December 1894
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Pr%C3%A9lude%20%C3%A0%20l%27apr%C3%A8s-midi%20d%27un%20faune%20Claude%20Debussy)
- **Links:** [Free scores on IMSLP (search)](https://imslp.org/index.php?search=Pr%C3%A9lude+%C3%A0+l%27apr%C3%A8s-midi+d%27un+faune+Debussy)

Debussy's Prelude to the Afternoon of a Faun was inspired by Stéphane Mallarmé's poem, in which a faun, half man and half goat, wakes in the heat of the afternoon and recalls, or dreams of, an encounter with nymphs. Debussy intended it as the first part of a triptych, but completed only the prelude. It opens with a sinuous, chromatic solo flute melody that slides through the interval of a tritone, unaccompanied, followed by soft harps and horns. The music develops not through traditional thematic argument but by subtle variation of colour and harmony, with flexible rhythm and a fluid form. Mallarmé was delighted. The conductor and composer Pierre Boulez said that modern music was awakened by it. In 1912 Vaslav Nijinsky choreographed it for the Ballets Russes, causing a scandal with its erotic ending.

### Pelléas et Mélisande (1893–1902)

- **Date:** 1893–1902
- **Type:** Opera (drame lyrique), five acts
- **Premiere / first published:** Opéra-Comique, Paris, 30 April 1902
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Pell%C3%A9as%20et%20M%C3%A9lisande%20Claude%20Debussy)
- **Links:** [Free scores on IMSLP (search)](https://imslp.org/index.php?search=Pell%C3%A9as+et+M%C3%A9lisande+Debussy)

Debussy set Maurice Maeterlinck's Symbolist play almost word for word. In the mysterious kingdom of Allemonde, Prince Golaud finds the fragile Mélisande weeping by a spring in the forest and marries her; she and his younger half-brother Pelléas fall in love, and Golaud in jealousy kills Pelléas. Mélisande dies after giving birth. Instead of arias, the singers follow the natural inflections of French speech, and the orchestra creates atmosphere through subtle colours, while the most intense moments are often almost silent. Maeterlinck, who had wanted his mistress to sing the title role, publicly hoped for the opera's failure. The premiere divided critics but it soon became a cult among young musicians. It is a key work of operatic modernism.

### La mer (1903–05)

- **Date:** 1903–05
- **Type:** Three symphonic sketches for orchestra
- **Premiere / first published:** Concerts Lamoureux, Paris, 15 October 1905
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=La%20mer%20Claude%20Debussy)
- **Links:** [Free scores on IMSLP (search)](https://imslp.org/index.php?search=La+mer+Debussy)

Debussy wrote La mer mainly in Burgundy and Jersey, far from the sea, which he said he preferred to know through memory. Its three movements are From Dawn to Noon on the Sea, Play of the Waves and Dialogue of the Wind and the Sea. Rather than painting literal pictures, the music evokes the changing light, motion and power of the sea through constantly transforming motifs and orchestral textures. At his request, the cover of the first edition reproduced part of Hokusai's woodblock print The Great Wave off Kanagawa. The first performance was poorly received, partly due to the scandal of Debussy's divorce. It later became one of the most frequently performed orchestral works of the twentieth century.
