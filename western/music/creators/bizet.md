## Biography

Georges Bizet was born in Paris in 1838 into a musical family and entered the Paris Conservatoire before his tenth birthday. A brilliant pianist, he wrote the Symphony in C at seventeen and won the Prix de Rome in 1857. Back in Paris he struggled to establish himself in the theatre, producing Les pêcheurs de perles (1863) and La jolie fille de Perth, and earning a living from arrangements and teaching. In 1872 his incidental music for Daudet's play L'Arlésienne showed his maturity. Carmen opened at the Opéra-Comique in March 1875 to a mixed and partly scandalised reception. Bizet died of a heart attack three months later, aged thirty-six.

Bizet's music is marked by vivid melody, clear orchestration and dramatic directness. Carmen broke the conventions of the family-oriented Opéra-Comique with its working-class characters, sensuality and on-stage murder. Its Spanish colour draws on popular songs, and its realism anticipated Italian verismo. Within a few years the opera was triumphing across Europe; Tchaikovsky predicted it would become the most popular opera in the world, and Nietzsche held it up against Wagner.

## Key works

### Carmen (1873–74)

[![Carmen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d4/Prudent-Louis_Leray_-_Poster_for_the_premi%C3%A8re_of_Georges_Bizet%27s_Carmen.jpg/960px-Prudent-Louis_Leray_-_Poster_for_the_premi%C3%A8re_of_Georges_Bizet%27s_Carmen.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Prudent-Louis_Leray_-_Poster_for_the_premi%C3%A8re_of_Georges_Bizet%27s_Carmen.jpg)

- **Date:** 1873–74
- **Type:** Opéra comique, four acts
- **Premiere / first published:** Opéra-Comique, Paris, 3 March 1875
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Carmen%20Georges%20Bizet)
- **Links:** [Free scores on IMSLP (search)](https://imslp.org/index.php?search=Carmen+Bizet)

Carmen, with a libretto by Henri Meilhac and Ludovic Halévy after Prosper Mérimée's novella, is set in Seville. Carmen, a cigarette-factory worker, seduces the soldier Don José, who deserts the army to join her and a band of smugglers. When she leaves him for the bullfighter Escamillo, José stabs her to death outside the bullring. The Opéra-Comique was a family theatre, and its directors were shocked by the realism and immorality of the story, which some critics condemned. Bizet's score is full of memorable numbers, including Carmen's Habanera, based on a song by Sebastián Yradier, the Seguidilla and the Toreador Song, and Spanish rhythms that he largely invented himself. Bizet died three months after the premiere. A version with sung recitatives by Ernest Guiraud spread the opera worldwide, and it is one of the most performed operas.
