# Romantic & national history

*c. 1820–1870*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Nineteenth-century historians wrote vivid narrative histories of nations and peoples, often with a political purpose. Michelet told France's story as the life of its people, Macaulay celebrated English constitutional liberty, Carlyle dramatised revolution and heroism, and Tocqueville studied democracy as a social condition.
