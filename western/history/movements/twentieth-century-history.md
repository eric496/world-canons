# 20th-century history & human-rights documents

*c. 1890–1990*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

Twentieth-century historians brought race, class and empire to the centre of the discipline, from W. E. B. Du Bois to Eric Hobsbawm. After the Second World War, the Universal Declaration of Human Rights set out a common standard of rights for all peoples.

## Historians & authors

-  [W. E. B. Du Bois](../creators/du-bois.md) (1868 – 1963)
-  [Eric Hobsbawm](../creators/hobsbawm.md) (1917 – 2012)
-  [UN Commission on Human Rights](../creators/un-human-rights-commission.md) (1946 – 1948)
