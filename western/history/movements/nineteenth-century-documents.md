# Reform, emancipation & revolution

*1848–1865*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Mid-century movements for women's rights, for socialism and against slavery produced short texts of lasting force: the Seneca Falls Declaration of Sentiments, the Communist Manifesto, and Lincoln's Emancipation Proclamation and Gettysburg Address.
