# Professional & scientific history

*c. 1824–1900*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

In German universities history became a discipline with seminars, archives and footnotes. Ranke insisted on primary sources and on understanding each age in its own terms; Mommsen combined epigraphy and narrative; Acton promoted the ideal of impartial, collaborative scholarship. The model spread across Europe and America.
