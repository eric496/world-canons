# Enlightenment history

*c. 1730–1790*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

Enlightenment historians widened history from kings and battles to manners, commerce, religion and ideas, and wrote it as a critical, often ironic, story of human progress and folly. Voltaire, Hume and Gibbon made it a bestselling literary form, read across Europe.

## Historians & authors

-  [Voltaire (François-Marie Arouet)](../creators/voltaire.md) (1694 – 1778)
-  [David Hume](../creators/hume.md) (1711 – 1776)
-  [Edward Gibbon](../creators/gibbon.md) (1737 – 1794)
