# Roman historiography & biography

*c. 60 BC – AD 200*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

Roman writers treated history as a branch of literature and moral instruction. Caesar wrote self-justifying war commentaries, Sallust and Tacitus anatomised political corruption, Livy told the whole story of the city from its foundation, and Suetonius and Plutarch made biography a major form. Greek-speaking subjects of the empire, such as Josephus and Arrian, wrote alongside them.
