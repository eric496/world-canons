# Medieval chronicles & charters

*c. 700–1400*

Era: [The Middle Ages](../../periods/middle-ages.md)

Medieval history was written mostly in monasteries and courts: annals kept year by year, lives of rulers and saints, and grand chronicles of nations and crusades. Bede, Einhard, Anna Komnene, Matthew Paris and Froissart are among its masters. The period also produced records and charters, from Domesday Book to Magna Carta, that historians still mine and that shaped later law.
