# Greek historiography

*c. 440–140 BC*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

History as a written inquiry begins in fifth-century Greece. Herodotus collected stories from across the known world to explain the war between Greeks and Persians; Thucydides narrowed the lens to a single war and to causes he could verify. Xenophon and, later, Polybius extended the tradition, the latter to explain Rome's rise to world power.
