# Renaissance humanist history

*c. 1400–1560*

Era: [The Renaissance](../../periods/renaissance.md)

Italian humanists modelled their histories on Livy and Thucydides, wrote about their own city-states in a secular, political voice and began to criticise sources. Leonardo Bruni divided history into ancient, medieval and modern; Lorenzo Valla proved a famous document a forgery; Machiavelli and Guicciardini analysed power with a new realism.
