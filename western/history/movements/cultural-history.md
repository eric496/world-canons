# Cultural history

*c. 1860–1940*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Some historians turned from politics to the whole culture of an age: its art, festivals, beliefs and habits of mind. Burckhardt's portrait of Renaissance Italy and Huizinga's of late-medieval Burgundy are the classic examples of this approach.

## Historians & authors

-  [Jacob Burckhardt](../creators/burckhardt.md) (1818 – 1897)
-  [Johan Huizinga](../creators/huizinga.md) (1872 – 1945)
