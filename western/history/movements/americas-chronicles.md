# Chronicles of the Americas

*c. 1520–1600*

Era: [The Renaissance](../../periods/renaissance.md)

The Spanish conquest of the Americas produced a large literature of eyewitness narrative, justification and protest. Conquistadors such as Bernal Díaz recorded their campaigns, while the Dominican friar Bartolomé de las Casas denounced the violence done to Indigenous peoples. These works raised questions about conquest and rights that are still debated.

## Historians & authors
