# Late antique & church history

*c. 300–600*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

As the empire became Christian, Eusebius invented a new kind of history: the story of the Church, built on quoted documents. Ammianus Marcellinus wrote the last great Latin history in the classical manner, and Procopius chronicled Justinian's wars while secretly attacking him. Gregory of Tours carried the tradition into the Frankish kingdoms.
