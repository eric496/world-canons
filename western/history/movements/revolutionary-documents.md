# Age of Revolutions documents

*1765–1804*

Era: [The Enlightenment](../../periods/enlightenment.md)

Between the American and Haitian revolutions, declarations, constitutions and pamphlets turned Enlightenment ideas of natural rights and popular sovereignty into political programmes. These texts, and the arguments against them, still frame debates about liberty, equality and the limits of government.
