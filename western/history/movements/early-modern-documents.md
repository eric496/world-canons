# Early-modern political history & documents

*c. 1590–1700*

Era: [Baroque & the Scientific Revolution](../../periods/seventeenth-century.md)

Religious wars and constitutional conflicts produced landmark settlements: the Edict of Nantes, the Peace of Westphalia, and the English Petition of Right, Habeas Corpus Act and Bill of Rights. The same struggles produced histories written by participants, above all Clarendon's account of the English Civil War.
