# Annales school

*c. 1929–1970*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

Founded around the journal Annales by Marc Bloch and Lucien Febvre in 1929, this French school rejected narrow political narrative in favour of 'total history': geography, economy, society and mentalities over long periods. Fernand Braudel's study of the Mediterranean is its best-known achievement.

## Historians & authors

-  [Marc Bloch](../creators/bloch.md) (1886 – 1944)
-  [Fernand Braudel](../creators/braudel.md) (1902 – 1985)
