# Classical Antiquity (Greece & Rome)

*c. 500 BC – AD 100*

Era: [Classical Antiquity](../../periods/antiquity.md)

Greek sculptors develop the idealised, naturalistic human body, contrapposto and canons of proportion. Most Greek bronzes are lost and survive through Roman marble copies, which shaped every later classical revival.

## Artists

-  [Phidias](../creators/phidias.md) (c. 480 BC – c. 430 BC)
-  [Myron](../creators/myron.md) (active c. 470 BC – c. 440 BC)
-  [Polykleitos](../creators/polykleitos.md) (active c. 450 BC – c. 420 BC)
-  [Praxiteles](../creators/praxiteles.md) (active c. 375 BC – c. 330 BC)
-  [Agesander, Athenodoros & Polydorus](../creators/laocoon-sculptors.md) (active 1st century BC – –)
