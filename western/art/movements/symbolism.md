# Symbolism & Art Nouveau

*c. 1880–1910*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Dream, myth, eroticism and anxiety expressed through ornament and symbolic form; Vienna Secession and Nordic angst.
