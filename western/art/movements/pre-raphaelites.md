# Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood

*c. 1848–1870*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Young English painters reject academic convention for brilliant colour, minute natural detail and medieval, literary and moral subjects.
