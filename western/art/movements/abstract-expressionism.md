# Abstract Expressionism

*c. 1943–1960*

Era: [Since 1945](../../periods/contemporary.md)

New York becomes the art capital; gesture (Pollock) and colour field (Rothko) on monumental canvases.

## Artists

-  [Mark Rothko](../creators/rothko.md) (1903 – 1970)
-  [Willem de Kooning](../creators/de-kooning.md) (1904 – 1997)
-  [Jackson Pollock](../creators/pollock.md) (1912 – 1956)
