# American Modernism

*c. 1910–1960*

Era: [Modernism](../../periods/modern.md)

American artists portray the modern city and its isolation in a clear, realist idiom.

## Artists

-  [Edward Hopper](../creators/hopper.md) (1882 – 1967)
-  [Georgia O'Keeffe](../creators/okeeffe.md) (1887 – 1986)
-  [Grant Wood](../creators/grant-wood.md) (1891 – 1942)
