# Post-Impressionism

*c. 1886–1905*

Era: [Romanticism to the Fin de Siècle](../../periods/nineteenth-century.md)

Painters push past Impressionism toward structure (Cézanne), science (Seurat), symbolism (Gauguin) and expressive colour (Van Gogh).
