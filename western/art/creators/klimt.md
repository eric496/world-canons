# Gustav Klimt

**1862 – 1918** · Austrian  
Born: Baumgarten, near Vienna · Died: Vienna  
Movements: [Symbolism & Art Nouveau](../movements/symbolism.md)  

> Co-founder and first president of the Vienna Secession. His 'golden phase' combined gold leaf, ornament and erotic symbolism.

## Biography

Gustav Klimt was born in Baumgarten near Vienna, the son of a gold engraver. He studied at the Vienna School of Arts and Crafts and, with his brother Ernst and Franz Matsch, formed a successful firm decorating public buildings, including the Burgtheater and the Kunsthistorisches Museum. In 1897 he led a group of young artists who broke away from the conservative establishment to found the Vienna Secession, and he became its first president. His ceiling paintings for the University of Vienna were attacked as pornographic and were later destroyed by fire in 1945. He spent summers at the Attersee painting landscapes with his lifelong companion, the fashion designer Emilie Flöge. He died in 1918 after a stroke, during the influenza pandemic.

Klimt's 'golden phase', influenced partly by the Byzantine mosaics he saw in Ravenna in 1903, combined realistically painted faces and hands with flat, ornamental surfaces of gold leaf and pattern. His themes were love, sexuality, life and death, and the female body. He was a central figure of Viennese Art Nouveau (Jugendstil) and mentored the young Egon Schiele. He also made thousands of erotic drawings.

### Portrait of Adele Bloch-Bauer I (1907)

[![Portrait of Adele Bloch-Bauer I](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/18/Gustav_Klimt%2C_1907%2C_Adele_Bloch-Bauer_I%2C_Neue_Galerie_New_York.jpg/1280px-Gustav_Klimt%2C_1907%2C_Adele_Bloch-Bauer_I%2C_Neue_Galerie_New_York.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gustav_Klimt%2C_1907%2C_Adele_Bloch-Bauer_I%2C_Neue_Galerie_New_York.jpg)

<sub>Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gustav_Klimt%2C_1907%2C_Adele_Bloch-Bauer_I%2C_Neue_Galerie_New_York.jpg).</sub>

- **Date:** 1907
- **Type:** Oil, silver and gold on canvas
- **Where to see it:** Neue Galerie, New York
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Portrait%20of%20Adele%20Bloch-Bauer%20I%20Gustav%20Klimt)

Klimt painted Adele Bloch-Bauer, the wife of the wealthy Jewish sugar industrialist Ferdinand Bloch-Bauer and a leading figure of Viennese society, in 1907, at the height of his golden phase. Her face, shoulders and hands are painted realistically, but her body and the background are dissolved into a dazzling surface of gold and silver leaf, decorated with Egyptian eye motifs, spirals and symbols. After the Nazis annexed Austria in 1938, the painting was seized and later displayed in the Austrian Gallery Belvedere. Adele's niece, Maria Altmann, fought for its return in a case that reached the US Supreme Court in 2004, and an arbitration panel ruled in her favour in 2006. Ronald Lauder then bought it for the Neue Galerie in New York. The story was told in the 2015 film Woman in Gold.

### The Kiss (1907–08)

[![The Kiss](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/40/The_Kiss_-_Gustav_Klimt_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg/1280px-The_Kiss_-_Gustav_Klimt_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Kiss_-_Gustav_Klimt_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg)

<sub>Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Kiss_-_Gustav_Klimt_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg).</sub>

- **Date:** 1907–08
- **Type:** Oil and gold leaf on canvas
- **Where to see it:** Österreichische Galerie Belvedere, Vienna
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=The%20Kiss%20Gustav%20Klimt)

Painted in 1907–08, The Kiss shows a couple locked in an embrace at the edge of a flowery meadow that ends abruptly at a cliff. The man, his face hidden, bends to kiss the woman's cheek. She kneels with her eyes closed, her hand around his neck. They are enveloped in a single golden robe: his decorated with black and white rectangles, hers with colourful circles and flowers, symbolising male and female. The Austrian state bought the painting in 1908 at the Kunstschau exhibition, even before it was finished. It is the most famous work of Viennese Art Nouveau and the highlight of the Belvedere in Vienna.
