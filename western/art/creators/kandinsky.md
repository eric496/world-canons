# Wassily Kandinsky

**1866 – 1944** · Russian-French  
Born: Moscow, Russia · Died: Neuilly-sur-Seine, France  
Movements: [Early Abstraction](../movements/abstraction.md), [Fauvism & Expressionism](../movements/fauvism-expressionism.md)  

> A pioneer of abstract painting who compared colour to music. He co-founded Der Blaue Reiter and taught at the Bauhaus.

## Biography

Wassily Kandinsky was born in Moscow and trained in law and economics, becoming a lecturer at Moscow University. He recalled being overwhelmed by one of Monet's Haystacks at an 1896 exhibition and by a performance of Wagner's Lohengrin. At 30 he gave up his academic career and moved to Munich to study painting. With Gabriele Münter he painted in the Bavarian village of Murnau. In 1911 he published Concerning the Spiritual in Art and founded the Blue Rider (Der Blaue Reiter) group with Franz Marc. He returned to Russia during the First World War and the Revolution, then taught at the Bauhaus in Germany from 1922 to 1933, publishing Point and Line to Plane (1926). When the Nazis closed the Bauhaus he moved to Neuilly-sur-Seine near Paris, where he died in 1944.

Kandinsky was one of the pioneers of abstract painting. He believed colour and form could act directly on the soul like music, and he grouped his works as Impressions, Improvisations and Compositions, terms borrowed from music. His style moved from the expressive, colourful abstractions of the Munich years to the geometric forms of the Bauhaus period and the biomorphic, playful works of his last decade in Paris.

## Key artworks

### Composition VII (1913)

<sub>Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Composition_VII_-_Wassily_Kandinsky%2C_GAC.jpg).</sub>

- **Date:** 1913
- **Type:** Oil on canvas
- **Where to see it:** State Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Composition%20VII%20Wassily%20Kandinsky)

Kandinsky regarded his ten Compositions, painted between 1910 and 1939, as his most important works, carefully prepared summations of his ideas. Composition VII, painted in Munich in 1913 after more than 30 preparatory drawings, watercolours and oil studies, is about 2 by 3 metres. It is a whirlwind of colour, lines and forms in which traces of biblical themes such as the Resurrection, the Last Judgement, the Flood and the Garden of Love have been almost entirely dissolved into abstraction. Kandinsky is said to have painted the final canvas in about four days. It is in the State Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow.
