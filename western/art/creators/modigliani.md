# Amedeo Modigliani

**1884 – 1920** · Italian  
Born: Livorno, Italy · Died: Paris  
Movements: [Fauvism & Expressionism](../movements/fauvism-expressionism.md)  

> A Paris-based painter and sculptor whose elongated faces and nudes draw on African and archaic sculpture. He died at 35.

## Biography

Amedeo Modigliani was born in Livorno, Italy, to a Sephardic Jewish family. Often ill with tuberculosis as a youth, he studied art in Florence and Venice and moved to Paris in 1906, living first in Montmartre and then in Montparnasse. Under the influence of Brâncuși and African and archaic sculpture, he devoted himself to carving stone heads between about 1909 and 1914, but the dust and cost forced him back to painting. He painted portraits of his friends, including Chaim Soutine, Juan Gris, Jean Cocteau and his dealer Léopold Zborowski, and of his companion Jeanne Hébuterne. His only solo exhibition in his lifetime, at the Berthe Weill gallery in 1917, was closed by the police on opening day because of its nudes. Weakened by poverty, alcohol and drugs, he died of tubercular meningitis in Paris in January 1920, aged 35.

Modigliani's style is instantly recognisable: elongated faces and necks, almond-shaped eyes often left blank, simplified, sculptural forms, warm colour and flowing outlines. His legend of bohemian poverty and early death has made him a symbol of the Paris art world, and his paintings are now among the most expensive in the world.

## Key artworks

### Nu couché (Reclining Nude) (1917–18)

[![Nu couché (Reclining Nude)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/2f/Modigliani_-_Nu_couch%C3%A9.jpg/1280px-Modigliani_-_Nu_couch%C3%A9.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Modigliani_-_Nu_couch%C3%A9.jpg)

<sub>Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Modigliani_-_Nu_couch%C3%A9.jpg).</sub>

- **Date:** 1917–18
- **Type:** Oil on canvas
- **Where to see it:** Private collection
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Nu%20couch%C3%A9%20%28Reclining%20Nude%29%20Amedeo%20Modigliani)

Painted in 1917–18, this large painting belongs to the series of reclining nudes that Modigliani made for his dealer Léopold Zborowski. Some of these nudes caused the police to close his only solo exhibition in 1917. A woman with a pale, elongated face and closed eyes lies across the whole width of the canvas, her warm, glowing body set against dark red and blue drapery. It draws on the tradition of Giorgione, Titian, Ingres and Manet, but with a frank, modern sensuality. In 2015 it sold at auction for $170.4 million to the Chinese collector Liu Yiqian, one of the highest prices ever paid for a painting at auction.
