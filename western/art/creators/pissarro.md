# Camille Pissarro

**1830 – 1903** · Danish-French  
Born: Charlotte Amalie, St Thomas (Danish West Indies) · Died: Paris  
Movements: [Impressionism](../movements/impressionism.md)  

> The only artist to exhibit in all eight Impressionist exhibitions and a generous mentor to Cézanne and Gauguin.

## Biography

Camille Pissarro was born on the Caribbean island of St Thomas, then a Danish colony, to a Sephardic Jewish family of French and Portuguese origin. After working in the family store he moved to Paris in 1855, where he was influenced by Corot. During the Franco-Prussian War of 1870–71 he took refuge in London, studied Constable and Turner, and met the dealer Paul Durand-Ruel. The Prussian army occupied his house in Louveciennes and destroyed many of his paintings. Back in France he lived in Pontoise, where he painted side by side with Cézanne, and later in Éragny. He was the only artist to show at all eight Impressionist exhibitions.

Pissarro was the patriarch and teacher of Impressionism: generous, open-minded and politically committed to anarchism. He mentored Cézanne, Gauguin and others. He painted rural landscapes, markets and peasants, and from 1885 to 1890 he experimented with the dot technique of Seurat's Neo-Impressionism. In his last years an eye infection forced him to work indoors, and he painted series of Paris, Rouen and Le Havre from hotel windows. His sons, including Lucien, also became painters.

## Key artworks

### Boulevard Montmartre at Night (1897)

[![Boulevard Montmartre at Night](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/14/Camille_Pissarro_-_Boulevard_Montmartre_at_Night_-_c_1897_-_National_Gallery_UK.jpg/1280px-Camille_Pissarro_-_Boulevard_Montmartre_at_Night_-_c_1897_-_National_Gallery_UK.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Camille_Pissarro_-_Boulevard_Montmartre_at_Night_-_c_1897_-_National_Gallery_UK.jpg)

<sub>Image: Wikimedia Commons. [Full resolution, credit and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Camille_Pissarro_-_Boulevard_Montmartre_at_Night_-_c_1897_-_National_Gallery_UK.jpg).</sub>

- **Date:** 1897
- **Type:** Oil on canvas
- **Where to see it:** National Gallery, London
- **Read more:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?search=Boulevard%20Montmartre%20at%20Night%20Camille%20Pissarro)

In 1897 Pissarro rented a room in the Grand Hôtel de Russie in Paris overlooking the busy Boulevard Montmartre. From its window he painted a series of views of the boulevard in different weather and at different times of day. This is the only night scene in the series. Wet pavements reflect the yellowish glow of the shop windows and the newly installed electric street lights, while carriages and pedestrians become flickering dabs of paint. It is one of the first great paintings of a city at night. It is in the National Gallery, London.
